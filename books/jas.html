2:10 quicumque autem totam legem servaverit offendat autem in uno factus est omnium reusAnd whosoever shall keep the whole Law, but offendeth in one, is made guilty of all.
2:14 quid proderit fratres meiWhat shall it profit, my brethren, if a man say he hath faith, but hath not works? Shall faith be able to save him? si fidem quis dicat se habere opera autem non habeat
3:5 ecce quantus ignis quam magnam silvam incenditSo the tongue also is certes a little member, and vaunteth great things. Behold how much fire what a great wood it kindleth?
3:6 et lingua ignis est universitas iniquitatisAnd the tongue, is fire, a whole world of iniquity. The tongue is set among our members, which defileth the whole body, and inflameth the wheel of our nativity, inflamed of hell.
3:10 non oportet fratres mei haec ita fieriOut of the self-same mouth proceedeth blessing &; cursing. These things must not be so done, my brethren.
4:16 nunc autem exultatis in superbiis vestrisBut now you rejoice in your arrogancies. All such rejoicing is wicked.
5:3 thesaurizastis in novissimis diebusYour gold and silver is rusted; and their rust shall be for a testimony to you, and shall eat your flesh as fire. You have stored to yourselves wrath in the last days.
