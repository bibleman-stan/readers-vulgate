1:1 Paulus apostolus Christi Iesu per voluntatem Dei sanctis omnibus qui sunt Ephesi et fidelibus in Christo IesuPaul an Apostle of Jesus Christ by the will of God: to all the saints that are at Ephesus; &; to the faithful in Christ Jesus.
1:2 gratia vobis et pax a Deo Patre nostro et Domino Iesu ChristoGrace to you and peace from God our Father, and our Lord Jesus Christ.
1:3 benedictus Deus et Pater Domini nostri Iesu Christi qui benedixit nos in omni benedictione spiritali in caelestibus in ChristoBlessed be God and the Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, which hath blessed us in all spiritual blessing, in celestials, in Christ: sicut elegit nos in ipso ante mundi constitutionem ut essemus sancti et inmaculati in conspectu eius in caritateas he chose us in him before the constitution of the world, that we should be holy and immaculate in his sight in charity.
1:5 qui praedestinavit nos in adoptionem filiorum per Iesum Christum in ipsum secundum propositum voluntatis suae in laudem gloriae gratiae suae in qua gratificavit nos in dilectoWho hath predestinated us unto the adoption of sons, by Jesus Christ, unto himself; according to the purpose of his will: unto the praise of the glory of his grace, wherein he hath gratified us in his beloved Son.
1:7 in quo habemus redemptionem per sanguinem eius remissionem peccatorum secundum divitias gratiae eius quae superabundavit in nobis in omni sapientia et prudentiaIn whom we have redemption by his blood (the remission of sins) according to the riches of his grace. ut notum faceret nobis sacramentum voluntatis suae secundum bonum placitum eius quod proposuit in eo in dispensationem plenitudinis temporum instaurare omnia in Christo quae in caelis et quae in terra sunt in ipsoWhich hath superabounded in us in all wisdom and prudence, that he might make known unto us the sacrament of his will, according to his good pleasure, which he purposed in himself, in the dispensation of the fullness of times, to perfit all things in Christ, that are in heaven and in earth, in him.
1:11 in quo etiam sorte vocati sumus praedestinati secundum propositum eius qui omnia operatur secundum consilium voluntatis suae ut simus in laudem gloriae eius qui ante speravimus in ChristoIn whom we also are called by lot: predestinate according to the purpose of him that worketh all things, according to the counsel of his will: that we may be unto the praise of his glory, which before have hoped in Christ:
1:13 in quo et vos cum audissetis verbum veritatis evangelium salutis vestrae in quo et credentes signati estis Spiritu promissionis Sancto qui est pignus hereditatis nostrae in redemptionem adquisitionis in laudem gloriae ipsiusin whom you also, when you had heard the word of truth (the Gospel of your salvation) in which also believing you were signed with the holy Spirit of promise, which is the pledge of our inheritance, to the redemption of acquisition, unto the praise of his glory.
1:15 propterea et ego audiens fidem vestram quae est in Domino Iesu et dilectionem in omnes sanctos non cesso gratias agens pro vobis memoriam vestri faciens in orationibus meisTherefore I also hearing your faith that is in our Lord Jesus, and love toward all the saints, cease not to give thanks for you, making a memory of you in my prayers, that God of our Lord Jesus Christ, ut Deus Domini nostri Iesu Christi Pater gloriae det vobis spiritum sapientiae et revelationis in agnitione eiusthe Father of glory, give you the spirit of wisdom and of revelation, in the knowledge of him,
1:18 inluminatos oculos cordis vestrithe eyes of your heart illuminated, ut sciatis quae sit spes vocationis eius quae divitiae gloriae hereditatis eius in sanctisthat you may know what the hope is of his vocation, and what are the riches of the glory of his inheritance in the saints, et quae sit supereminens magnitudo virtutis eius in nos qui credidimus secundum operationem potentiae virtutis eius quam operatus est in Christo suscitans illum a mortuis et constituens ad dexteram suam in caelestibus supra omnem principatum et potestatem et virtutem et dominationem et omne nomen quod nominatur non solum in hoc saeculo sed et in futuroand what is the passing greatness of his power toward us that believe: according to the operation of the might of his power, which he wrought in Christ, raising him up from the dead, and setting him on his right hand in celestials above all Principality &; Potestate &; Power, and Domination, &; every name that is named not only in this world, but also in that to come.
1:22 et omnia subiecit sub pedibus eiusAnd he hath subdued all things under his feet: et ipsum dedit caput supra omnia ecclesiae quae est corpus ipsius plenitudo eius qui omnia in omnibus adimpleturand hath made him Head over all the Church, which is his body, the fullness of him, which is filled all in all.
2:1 et vos cum essetis mortui delictis et peccatis vestris in quibus aliquando ambulastis secundum saeculum mundi huius secundum principem potestatis aeris huius spiritus qui nunc operatur in filios diffidentiae in quibus et nos omnes aliquando conversati sumus in desideriis carnis nostrae facientes voluntates carnis et cogitationum et eramus natura filii irae sicut et ceteriAnd you when you were dead by your offences and sins, wherein sometime you walked according to the course of this world, according to the prince of the power of this air, of the spirit that now worketh on the children of diffidence, in whom also we all conversed sometime in the desires of our flesh, doing the will of the flesh and of thoughts, and were by nature the children of wrath as also the rest: but God (which is rich in mercy) for his exceeding charity wherewith he loved us, even when we were dead by sins, quickened us together in Christ, (by whose grace you are saved,) and raised us up with him, and hath made us sit with him in the celestials in Christ Jesus, that he might shew in the worlds succeeding, the aboundant riches of his grace, in bounty upon us in Christ Jesus.
2:8 gratia enim estis salvati per fidem et hoc non ex vobisFor by grace you are saved through faith (and that not of yourselves, Dei enim donum estfor it is the gift of God)
2:9 non ex operibusnot of works, ut ne quis glorieturthat no man glory.
2:10 ipsius enim sumus factura creati in Christo Iesu in operibus bonis quae praeparavit DeusFor we are his work, created in Christ Jesus in good works, ut in illis ambulemuswhich God hath prepared that we should walk in them.
2:11 propter quod memores estote quod aliquando vos gentes in carne qui dicimini praeputium ab ea quae dicitur circumcisio in carne manufactaFor the which cause be mindful that sometime you were Gentiles in the flesh, who were called prepuce, of that which is called circumcision in the flesh, made with hand:
2:12 quia eratis illo in tempore sine Christo alienati a conversatione Israhel et hospites testamentorum promissionis spem non habentes et sine Deo in mundowho were at that time without Christ, alienated from the conversation of Israel, and strangers of the testaments, having no hope of the promise, and without God in this world.
2:13 nunc autem in Christo Iesu vos qui aliquando eratis longe facti estis prope in sanguine ChristiBut now in Christ Jesus, you that sometime were far off, are made nigh in the blood of Christ.
2:14 ipse est enim pax nostra qui fecit utraque unumFor he is our peace, who hath made both one, et medium parietem maceriae solvens inimicitiam in carne sua legem mandatorum decretis evacuansand dissolving the middle wall of the partition, the enmities in his flesh: ut duos condat in se met ipsum in unum novum hominem faciens pacemevacuating the law of commandments in decrees: that he may create the two in himself into one new man, et reconciliet ambos in uno corpore Deo per crucem interficiens inimicitiam in se met ipsomaking peace, and may reconcile both in one body to God by the cross, killing the enmities in himself. et veniens evangelizavit pacem vobis qui longe fuistis et pacem his qui propeAnd coming he evangelized peace to you that were far off, and peace to them that were nigh.
2:18 quoniam per ipsum habemus accessum ambo in uno Spiritu ad PatremFor by him we have access both in one Spirit to the Father.
2:19 ergo iam non estis hospites et advenaeNow then you are not strangers and foreigners: sed estis cives sanctorum et domestici Dei superaedificati super fundamentum apostolorum et prophetarum ipso summo angulari lapide Christo Iesubut you are citizens of the saints, and the domesticals of God, built upon the foundation of the Apostles and Prophets, Jesus Christ himself being the highest corner-stone:
2:21 in quo omnis aedificatio constructa crescit in templum sanctum in Domino in quo et vos coaedificamini in habitaculum Dei in Spirituin whom all building framed together, groweth into an holy temple in our Lord, in whom you also are built together into an habitation of God in the Holy Ghost.
3:1 huius rei gratia ego Paulus vinctus Christi Iesu pro vobis gentibusFor this cause, I Paul the prisoner of Jesus Christ, for you Gentiles: if yet you have heard the dispensation of the grace of God, which is given me toward you. because according to revelation the sacrament was made known to me, as I have written before in brief: according as you reading may understand my wisdom in the mystery of Christ, which unto other generations was not known to the sons of men, as now it is revealed to his holy Apostles, and Prophets in the Spirit. The Gentiles to be coheirs and concorporate and comparticipant of his promise in Christ Jesus by the Gospel:
3:7 cuius factus sum minister secundum donum gratiae Dei quae data est mihi secundum operationem virtutis eiuswhereof I am made a minister according to the gift of the grace of God, which is given me according to the operation of his power.
3:8 mihi omnium sanctorum minimo data est gratia haec in gentibus evangelizare investigabiles divitias Christi et inluminare omnes quae sit dispensatio sacramenti absconditi a saeculis in Deo qui omnia creavitTo me the least of all the saints is given this grace, among the Gentiles to evangelize the unsearchable riches of Christ, &; to illuminate all men what is the dispensation of the sacrament hidden from worlds in God, who created all things: ut innotescat principibus et potestatibus in caelestibus per ecclesiam multiformis sapientia Dei secundum praefinitionem saeculorum quam fecit in Christo Iesu Domino nostro in quo habemus fiduciam et accessum in confidentia per fidem eiusthat the manifold wisdom of God, may be notified to the Princes &; Potestates in the celestials by the Church, according to the prefinition of worlds, which he made in Christ Jesus our Lord. In whom we have affiance and access in confidence by the faith of him.
3:13 propter quod peto ne deficiatis in tribulationibus meis pro vobis quae est gloria vestraFor the which cause I desire that you faint not in my tribulations for you, which is your glory.
3:14 huius rei gratia flecto genua mea ad Patrem Domini nostri Iesu Christi ex quo omnis paternitas in caelis et in terra nominaturFor this cause I bow my knees to the Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, of whom all paternity in the Heavens and in earth is named, that he give you according to the riches of his glory, power to be fortified by his Spirit in the inner man. ut det vobis secundum divitias gloriae suae virtute corroborari per Spiritum eius in interiore homine habitare Christum per fidem in cordibus vestrisChrist to dwell by faith in your hearts, rooted and founded in charity, that you may be able to comprehend with all the saints, what is the breadth, in caritate radicati et fundati ut possitis conprehendere cum omnibus sanctis quae sit latitudo et longitudo et sublimitas et profundumand length, and height and depth, to know also the charity of Christ, scire etiam supereminentem scientiae caritatem Christisurpassing knowledge, ut impleamini in omnem plenitudinem Deithat you may be filled unto all the fullness of God.
3:20 ei autem qui potens est omnia facere superabundanter quam petimus aut intellegimus secundum virtutem quae operatur in nobis ipsi gloria in ecclesia et in Christo Iesu in omnes generationes saeculi saeculorumAnd to him that is able to do all things more aboundantly than we desire or understand, according to the power that worketh in us:
3:21 amento him be glory in the Church, and in Christ Jesus, unto all generations world without end. Amen.
4:1 obsecro itaque vos ego vinctus in Domino ut digne ambuletis vocatione qua vocati estis cum omni humilitate et mansuetudine cum patientia subportantes invicem in caritate solliciti servare unitatem spiritus in vinculo pacisI therefore prisoner in our Lord, beseech you, that you walk worthy of the vocation in which you are called, with all humility and mildness, with patience, supporting one another in charity, careful to keep the unity of the spirit in the bond of peace.
4:4 unum corpus et unus spiritus sicut vocati estis in una spe vocationis vestraeOne body &; one spirit: as you are called in one hope of your vocation.
4:5 unus Dominus una fides unum baptismaOne Lord, one faith, one baptism.
4:6 unus Deus et Pater omnium qui super omnes et per omnia et in omnibus nobisOne God and Father of all, which is over all, and by all, &; in all us.
4:7 unicuique autem nostrum data est gratia secundum mensuram donationis ChristiBut to every one of us is given grace according to the measure of the donation of Christ.
4:8 propter quod dicitFor the which he saith: ascendens in altum captivam duxit captivitatemAscending on high he led captivity captive: dedit dona hominibushe gave gifts to men.
4:9 quod autem ascendit quid est(And that he ascended, nisi quia et descendit primum in inferiores partes terraewhat is it, but because he descended also first into the inferior parts of the earth?
4:10 qui descendit ipse est et qui ascendit super omnes caelosHe that descended, the same is also he that is ascended above all the Heavens, that he might fill all things.) And he gave, some Apostles, &; some Prophets, &; other-some Evangelists, &; other-some Pastors &; Doctors, to the consummation of the saints, unto the work of the ministry, unto the edifying of the body of Christ: until we meet all into the unity of faith and knowledge of the Son of God into a perfect man, into the measure of the age of the fullness of Christ: that now we be not children wavering, and carried about with every wind of doctrine in the wickedness of men, in craftiness to the circumvention of error. ut impleret omniaBut doing the truth in charity, let us in all things grow in him, which is the Head, Christ:
4:16 ex quo totum corpus conpactum et conexum per omnem iuncturam subministrationis secundum operationem in mensuram uniuscuiusque membri augmentum corporis facit in aedificationem sui in caritateof whom the whole body being compact and knit together by all juncture of subministration, according to the operation in the measure of every member, maketh the increase of the body unto the edifying of it-self in charity.
4:17 hoc igitur dicoThis therefore I say and testify in our Lord: et testificor in Domino ut iam non ambuletisthat now you walk not as also the Gentiles walk in the vanity of their sense, sicut gentes ambulant in vanitate sensus sui tenebris obscuratum habentes intellectum alienati a vita Dei per ignorantiam quae est in illis propter caecitatem cordis ipsorum qui desperantes se met ipsos tradiderunt inpudicitiae in operationem inmunditiae omnis in avaritiahaving their understanding obscured with darkness, alienated from the life of God by the ignorance that is in them, because of the blindness of their heart, who despairing, have given up themselves to impudicity, unto the operation of all uncleanness, unto avarice.
4:20 vos autem non ita didicistis ChristumBut you have not so learned Christ: si tamen illum audistisif yet you have heard him, et in ipso edocti estis sicut est veritas in Iesu deponere vos secundum pristinam conversationem veterem hominem qui corrumpitur secundum desideria erroris&; have been taught in him, (as the truth is in Jesus.) Lay you away, according to the old conversation the old man, which is corrupted according to the desires of error.
4:23 renovamini autem spiritu mentis vestraeAnd be renewed in the spirit of your mind: et induite novum hominem qui secundum Deum creatus est in iustitia et sanctitate veritatisand put on the new man which according to God is created in justice, and holiness of the truth.
4:25 propter quod deponentes mendacium loquimini veritatem unusquisque cum proximo suo quoniam sumus invicem membraFor the which cause laying away lying, speak ye truth every one with is neighbour, because we are members one of another.
4:26 irasciminiBe angry and sin not. et nolite peccareLet not the sun go down upon your anger. sol non occidat super iracundiam vestram
4:27 nolite locum dare diaboloGive not place to the devil.
4:28 qui furabatur iam non fureturHe that stole, let him now not steal: magis autem laboret operando manibus quod bonum estbut rather let him labour in working with his hands that which is good, that he may have whence to give unto him that suffereth necessity. All naughty speech let it not proceed out of your mouth: ut habeat unde tribuat necessitatem patientibut if there be any good to the edifying of the faith, that it may give grace to the hearers.
4:30 et nolite contristare Spiritum Sanctum DeiAnd contristate not the holy Spirit of God: in quo signati estis in die redemptionisin which you are signed unto the day of redemption.
4:31 omnis amaritudo et ira et indignatio et clamor et blasphemia tollatur a vobis cum omni malitiaLet all bitterness, and anger, and indignation, and clamour, and blasphemy be taken away from you with all malice.
4:32 estote autem invicem benigni misericordesAnd be gentle one to another, donantes invicemmerciful, sicut et Deus in Christo donavit nobispardoning one another, as also God in Christ hath pardoned you.
5:1 estote ergo imitatores Dei sicut filii carissimiBe ye therefore followers of God, as most dear children: et ambulate in dilectioneand walk in love, as Christ also loved us, and delivered himself for us an oblation and host to God in an odour of sweetness. sicut et Christus dilexit nosBut fornication and all uncleanness, or avarice let it not so much as be named among you as it becometh Saints: et tradidit se ipsum pro nobis oblationem et hostiam Deo in odorem suavitatisor filthiness, or foolish talk, or scurrility, being to no purpose: but rather giving of thanks.
5:5 hoc enim scitote intellegentes quod omnis fornicator aut inmundus aut avarus quod est idolorum servitus non habet hereditatem in regno Christi et DeiFor understanding know you this, that no fornicator, or unclean, or covetous person (which is the service of idols) hath inheritance in the Kingdom of Christ and of God.
5:6 nemo vos seducat inanibus verbisLet no man seduce you with vain words. propter haec enim venit ira Dei in filios diffidentiaeFor, for these things cometh the anger of God upon the children of diffidence.
5:7 nolite ergo effici participes eorumBecome not therefore partakers with them.
5:8 eratis enim aliquando tenebraeFor you were sometime darkness, but now light in our Lord. nunc autem lux in DominoWalk as children of the light, (for the fruit of the light is in all goodness, and justice, and verity) proving what is well pleasing to God:
5:11 et nolite communicare operibus infructuosis tenebrarumand communicate not with the unfruitful works of darkness, magis autem et redarguitebut rather reprove them.
5:12 quae enim in occulto fiunt ab ipsis turpe est et dicereFor the things that are done of them in secret, it is shame even to speak.
5:13 omnia autem quae arguuntur a lumine manifestanturBut all things that are reproved, are manifested by the light. omne enim quod manifestatur lumen estFor all that is manifested, is light.
5:14 propter quod dicitFor the which cause he saith: surge qui dormisRise thou that sleepest, and arise from the dead: et exsurge a mortuisand Christ will illuminate thee. See therefore, brethren, how you walk warily. et inluminabit tibi ChristusNot as unwise, but as wise: redeeming the time, because the days are evil.
5:17 propterea nolite fieri inprudentes sed intellegentes quae sit voluntas DominiTherefore become not unwise, but understanding what is the will of God.
5:18 et nolite inebriari vino in quo est luxuriaAnd be not drunk with wine wherein is riotousness, but be filled with the Spirit, speaking to yourselves in Psalms &; hymns, and spiritual canticles, chanting and singing in your hearts to our Lord: sed implemini Spiritu loquentes vobis met ipsis in psalmis et hymnis et canticis spiritalibus cantantes et psallentes in cordibus vestris Domino gratias agentes semper pro omnibus in nomine Domini nostri Iesu Christi Deo et Patri subiecti invicem in timore Christigiving thanks always for all things, in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ to God and the Father. Subject one to another in the fear of Christ.
5:22 mulieres viris suis subditae sint sicut Domino quoniam vir caput est mulieris sicut Christus caput est ecclesiaeLet women be subject to their husbands, as to our Lord:
5:23 ipse salvator corporisbecause the man is the head of the woman: as Christ is the head of the Church. Himself, the Saviour of his body.
5:24 sed ut ecclesia subiecta est Christo ita et mulieres viris suis in omnibusBut as the Church is subject to Christ, so also the women to their husbands in all things.
5:25 viri diligite uxoresHusbands, sicut et Christus dilexit ecclesiamlove your wives, et se ipsum tradidit pro eaas Christ also loved the Church, and delivered himself for it: ut illam sanctificaret mundans lavacro aquae in verbothat he might sanctify it, cleansing it by the laver of water in the word, ut exhiberet ipse sibi gloriosam ecclesiam non habentem maculam aut rugam aut aliquid eius modi sed ut sit sancta et inmaculatathat he might present to himself a glorious Church, not having spot, or wrinkle, or any such thing, but that it may be holy and unspotted.
5:28 ita et viri debent diligere uxores suas ut corpora suaSo also men ought to love their wives as their own bodies. qui suam uxorem diligit se ipsum diligitHe that loveth his wife, loveth himself.
5:29 nemo enim umquam carnem suam odio habuitFor no man ever hated his own flesh: sed nutritbut he nourisheth and cherisheth it, et fovet eam sicut et Christus ecclesiamas also Christ the Church:
5:30 quia membra sumus corporis eius de carne eius et de ossibus eiusbecause we be the members of his body, of his flesh, and of his bones.
5:31 propter hoc relinquet homo patrem et matrem suamFor this cause shall man leave his father and mother: et adherebit uxori suaeand shall cleave to his wife, et erunt duo in carne unaand they shall be two in one flesh.
5:32 sacramentum hoc magnum estThis is a great Sacrament. ego autem dico in Christo et in ecclesiaBut I speak in Christ &; in the Church.
5:33 verumtamen et vos singuli unusquisque suam uxorem sicut se ipsum diligatNevertheless you also every one, let each love his wife as himself: uxor autem ut timeat virumand let the wife fear her husband.
6:1 filii oboedite parentibus vestris in DominoChildren, obey your parents in our Lord. hoc enim est iustumFor this is just.
6:2 honora patrem tuum et matremHonour thy father &; thy mother (which is the first commandment in the promise) that it may be well with thee, quod est mandatum primum in promissione&; thou mayest be long lived upon the earth. ut bene sit tibi et sis longevus super terram
6:4 et patres nolite ad iracundiam provocare filios vestrosAnd you fathers, provoke not your children to anger: sed educate illos in disciplina et correptione Dominibut bring them up in the discipline &; correption of our Lord.
6:5 servi oboedite dominis carnalibus cum timore et tremore in simplicitate cordis vestri sicut Christo non ad oculum servientes quasi hominibus placentes sed ut servi Christi facientes voluntatem Dei ex animo cum bona voluntate servientes sicut Domino et non hominibus scientes quoniam unusquisque quodcumque fecerit bonum hoc percipiet a Domino sive servus sive liberServants, be obedient to your lords according to the flesh, with fear and trembling, in the simplicity of your heart, as to Christ: not serving to the eye, as it were pleasing men, but as the servants of Christ, doing the will of God from the heart. with a good will serving, as to our Lord and not to men. Knowing that every one what good soever he shall do, that shall he receive of our Lord, whether he be bond, or free.
6:9 et domini eadem facite illis remittentes minas scientes quia et illorum et vester Dominus est in caelisAnd you masters, do the same things to them, remitting threatenings: knowing that both their Lord and yours, is in Heaven: et personarum acceptio non est apud eumand acception of persons is not with him.
6:10 de cetero fratres confortamini in Domino et in potentia virtutis eiusHence-forth, brethren, be strengthened in our Lord, and in the might of his power.
6:11 induite vos arma DeiPut you on the armour of God, ut possitis stare adversus insidias diabolithat you may stand against the deceits of the devil.
6:12 quia non est nobis conluctatio adversus carnem et sanguinem sed adversus principes et potestates adversus mundi rectores tenebrarum harum contra spiritalia nequitiae in caelestibusFor our wrestling is not against flesh and blood: but against Princes and Potestates, against the rectors of the world of this darkness, against the spirituals of wickedness in the celestials.
6:13 propterea accipite armaturam DeiTherefore take the armour of God, ut possitis resistere in die malo et omnibus perfectis starethat you may resist in the evil day, and stand in all things perfect.
6:14 state ergo succincti lumbos vestros in veritate et induti loricam iustitiae et calciati pedes in praeparatione evangelii pacis in omnibus sumentes scutum fidei in quo possitis omnia tela nequissimi ignea extinguereStand therefore having your loins girded in truth, and clothed with the breast-plate of justice, &; having your feet shod to the preparation of the Gospel of peace: in all things taking the shield of faith, wherewith you may extinguish all the fiery darts of the most wicked one.
6:17 et galeam salutis adsumite et gladium Spiritus quod est verbum Dei per omnem orationem et obsecrationem orantes omni tempore in Spiritu et in ipso vigilantes in omni instantia et obsecratione pro omnibus sanctis et pro meAnd take unto you the helmet of salvation: and the sword of the spirit (which is the word of God) in all prayer and supplication praying at all time in spirit: and in the same watching in all instance and supplication for all the saints: ut detur mihi sermo in apertione oris mei cum fiducia notum facere mysterium evangeliiand for me, that speech may be given me in the opening of my mouth with confidence, to make known the mystery of the Gospel,
6:20 pro quo legatione fungor in catena itafor the which I am a legate in this chain, ut in ipso audeamso that in it I may be bold according as I ought, prout oportet me loquito speak.
6:21 ut autem et vos sciatis quae circa me sunt quid agam omnia nota vobis faciet Tychicus carissimus frater et fidelis minister in DominoAnd that you also may know the things about me, what I do, Tychicus my dearest brother and faithful minister in our Lord, will make you understand all things:
6:22 quem misi ad vos in hoc ipsum ut cognoscatis quae circa nos suntwhom I have sent to you for this same purpose, that you may know the things about us, et consoletur corda vestraand he may comfort your hearts.
6:23 pax fratribus et caritas cum fide a Deo Patre et Domino Iesu ChristoPeace to the brethren and charity with faith from God the Father, and our Lord Jesus Christ.
6:24 gratia cum omnibus qui diligunt Dominum nostrum Iesum Christum in incorruptioneGrace with all that love our Lord Jesus Christ in incorruption. Amen.
