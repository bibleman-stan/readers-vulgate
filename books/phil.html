1:1 Paulus et Timotheus servi Iesu Christi omnibus sanctis in Christo Iesu qui sunt Philippis cum episcopis et diaconisPaul and Timothee the servants of Jesus Christ; to all the saints in Christ Jesus that are at Philippi, with the Bishops and Deacons.
1:2 gratia vobis et pax a Deo Patre nostro et Domino Iesu ChristoGrace to you and peace from God our Father, and our Lord Jesus Christ.
1:3 gratias ago Deo meo in omni memoria vestri semper in cunctis orationibus meis pro omnibus vobis cum gaudio deprecationem faciens super communicatione vestra in evangelio a prima die usque nunc confidens hoc ipsum quia qui coepit in vobis opus bonum perficiet usque in diem Christi IesuI give thanks to my God in all memory of you
1:7 sicut est mihi iustum hoc sentire pro omnibus vobis eoAs it is reason for me, this to think for all you, for that I have you in heart, &; in my bands, and in the defence, and the confirmation of the Gospel, all you to be partakers of my joy. quod habeam in corde vos et in vinculis meis et in defensione et confirmatione evangelii socios gaudii mei omnes vos esse
1:8 testis enim mihi est Deus quomodo cupiam omnes vos in visceribus Christi IesuFor God is my witness, how I covet you all in the bowels of Jesus Christ.
1:12 scire autem vos volo fratres quia quae circa me sunt magis ad profectum venerunt evangelii itaAnd I will have you know, brethren, that the things about me are come to the more furtherance of the Gospel: ut vincula mea manifesta fierent in Christo in omni praetorio et in ceteris omnibus et plures e fratribus in Domino confidentes vinculis meis abundantius audere sine timore verbum Dei loqui
1:15 quidam quidem et propter invidiam et contentionemSome indeed even for envy and contention: but some also for good will preach Christ. quidam autem et propter bonam voluntatem Christum praedicant
1:16 quidam ex caritate scientes quoniam in defensionem evangelii positus sumSome of charity: knowing that I am set unto the defence of the Gospel.
1:17 quidam autem ex contentione Christum adnuntiant non sincere existimantes pressuram se suscitare vinculis meisAnd some of contention preach Christ not sincerely: supposing that they raise affliction to my bands.
1:18 quid enim dum omni modo sive per occasionem sive per veritatem Christus adnuntiaturBut what? So that by all means, whether by occasion, or by truth, Christ be preached: in this also I rejoice, yea &; will rejoice. et in hoc gaudeo sed et gaudebo
1:21 mihi enim vivere Christus est et mori lucrumFor unto me, to live is Christ: and to die is gain.
1:22 quod si vivere in carne hic mihi fructus operis estAnd if to live in the flesh, this unto me be the fruit of the work, and what I shall choose I know not. et quid eligam ignoro
1:23 coartor autem e duobus desiderium habens dissolvi et cum Christo esse multo magis meliusAnd I am straitened of the two: having desire to be dissolved &; to be with Christ, a thing much more better.
1:24 permanere autem in carne magis necessarium est propter vosBut to abide in the flesh, necessary for you.
1:25 et hoc confidens scio quia manebo et permanebo omnibus vobis ad profectum vestrum et gaudium fideiAnd trusting this, I know that I shall abide and continue with you all, unto your furtherance and joy of the faith: ut gratulatio vestra abundet in Christo Iesu in me per meum adventum iterum ad vos
2:1 si qua ergo consolatio in Christo si quod solacium caritatis si qua societas spiritus si quid viscera et miserationes implete gaudium meumIF therefore there be any consolation in Christ, if any solace of charity, if any society of spirit, if any bowels of commiseration; ut idem sapiatis eandem caritatem habentes unianimes id ipsum sentientes nihil per contentionem ne que per inanem gloriam sed in humilitate superiores sibi invicem arbitrantes non quae sua sunt singuli considerantes sed et ea quae aliorum
2:5 hoc enim sentite in vobis quod et in Christo IesuFor this think in yourselves, which also in Christ Jesus;
2:6 qui cum in forma Dei esset non rapinam arbitratus est esse se aequalem Deowho when he was in the form of God, thought it no robbery, himself to be equal to God:
2:7 sed se met ipsum exinanivit formam servi accipiens in similitudinem hominum factus et habitu inventus ut homobut he exinanited himself, taking the form of a servant, made into the similitude of men, and in shape found as a man.
2:8 humiliavit se met ipsum factus oboediens usque ad mortem mortem autem crucisHe humbled himself, made obedient unto death: even the death of the cross.
2:9 propter quod et Deus illum exaltavitFor the which thing God also hath exalted him, and hath given him a name which is above all names: et donavit illi nomen super omne nomen ut in nomine Iesu omne genu flectat caelestium et terrestrium et infernorum et omnis lingua confiteatur quia Dominus Iesus Christus in gloria est Dei Patris
2:13 Deus est enim qui operatur in vobis et velle et perficere pro bona voluntateFor it is God that worketh in you both to will and to accomplish, according to his good will.
2:14 omnia autem facite sine murmurationibus et haesitationibus ut sitis sine querella et simplices filii Dei sine reprehensione in medio nationis pravae et perversae inter quos lucetis sicut luminaria in mundo verbum vitae continentes ad gloriam meam in die ChristiAnd do ye all things without murmurings and staggerings: quia non in vacuum cucurri ne que in vacuum laboravi
2:17 sed et si immolor supra sacrificium et obsequium fidei vestrae gaudeoBut and if I be immolated, upon the Sacrifice and service of your faith, I rejoice and congratulate with you all. et congratulor omnibus vobis
2:18 id ipsum autem et vos gaudeteAnd the self-same thing do you also rejoice, and congratulate with me. et congratulamini mihi
2:19 spero autem in Domino Iesu Timotheum cito me mittere ad vos ut et ego bono animo sim cognitis quae circa vos suntAnd I hope in our Lord Jesus, to send Timothee unto you quickly, that I also may be of good comfort, when I know the things pertaining to you.
2:20 neminem enim habeo tam unianimem qui sincera affectione pro vobis sollicitus sitFor I have no man so of one mind that with sincere affection is careful for you.
2:21 omnes enim sua quaeruntFor all seek the things that are their own; not the things that are Jesus Christ';s. non quae sunt Christi Iesu
2:22 experimentum autem eius cognoscite quoniam sicut patri filius me cum servivit in evangeliumAnd know ye an experiment of him, that as a son the Father, so hath he served with me in the Gospel.
2:23 hunc igitur spero me mittere moxThis man therefore I hope to send unto you, immediately as I shall see the things that concern me. ut videro quae circa me sunt
2:24 confido autem in Domino quoniam et ipse veniam ad vos citoAnd I trust in our Lord that myself also shall come to you quickly.
2:25 necessarium autem existimavi Epafroditum fratrem et cooperatorem et commilitonem meum vestrum autem apostolum et ministrum necessitatis meae mittere ad vosBut I have thought it necessary to send to you Epaphroditus my brother and coadjutor and fellow-soldier, but your Apostle, and minister of my necessity. quoniam quidem omnes vos desiderabat et maestus erat propterea quod audieratis illum infirmatum
2:27 nam et infirmatus est usque ad mortemFor indeed he was sick even to death: but God had mercy on him: and not only on him, but on me also, lest I should have sorrow upon sorrow. sed Deus misertus est eius non solum autem eius verum etiam et mei ne tristitiam super tristitiam haberem
2:28 festinantius ergo misi illumTherefore I sent him the more speedily: that seeing him, you may rejoice again, and I may be without sorrow. ut viso eo iterum gaudeatis et ego sine tristitia sim
2:29 excipite itaque illum cum omni gaudio in DominoReceive him therefore with all joy in our Lord: and such entreat with honour. et eius modi cum honore habetote quoniam propter opus Christi usque ad mortem accessit tradens animam suam ut impleret id quod ex vobis deerat erga meum obsequium
3:1 de cetero fratres mei gaudete in DominoFrom hence-forth, my brethren, rejoice in our Lord. To write the same things unto you, to me surely it is not tedious, and to you it is necessary. eadem vobis scribere mihi quidem non pigrum vobis autem necessarium
3:2 videte canesSee the dogs, see the evil workers, see the concision. videte malos operarios videte concisionem
3:3 nos enim sumus circumcisio qui spiritu Deo servimus et gloriamur in Christo IesuFor we are the circumcision, which in spirit serve God: and we glory in Christ Jesus, and not having confidence in the flesh, et non in carne fiduciam habentes quamquam ego habeam confidentiam et in carne
3:4 si quis alius videtur confidere in carne ego magis circumcisus octava die ex genere Israhel de tribu Beniamin Hebraeus ex Hebraeis secundum legem Pharisaeus secundum aemulationem persequens ecclesiam Dei secundum iustitiam quae in lege est conversatus sine querellaalbeit I also have confidence in the flesh. If any other man seem to have confidence in the flesh, I more,
3:7 sed quae mihi fuerunt lucra haec arbitratus sum propter Christum detrimentaBut the things that were gains to me, those have I esteemed for Christ, detriments.
3:8 verumtamen existimo omnia detrimentum esse propter eminentem scientiam Iesu Christi Domini mei propter quem omnia detrimentum feci et arbitror ut stercoraYea but I esteem all things to be detriment for the passing knowledge of Jesus Christ my Lord: for whom I have made all things as detriment, and do esteem them as dung, that I may gain Christ: ut Christum lucri faciam et inveniar in illo non habens meam iustitiam quae ex lege est sed illam quae ex fide est Christi quae ex Deo est iustitia in fide ad agnoscendum illum et virtutem resurrectionis eius et societatem passionum illius configuratus morti eius si quo modo occurram ad resurrectionem quae est ex mortuis
3:12 non quod iam acceperimNot that now I have received, or now am perfect: but I pursue, if I may comprehend, wherein I am also comprehended of Christ Jesus. aut iam perfectus sim sequor autem si conprehendam in quo et conprehensus sum a Christo Iesu
3:13 fratres ego me non arbitror conprehendisseBrethren, I do not account that I have comprehended. Yet one thing: forgetting the things that are behind, but stretching forth myself to those that are before, unum autem quae quidem retro sunt obliviscens ad ea vero quae sunt in priora extendens me ad destinatum persequor ad bravium supernae vocationis Dei in Christo Iesu
3:15 quicumque ergo perfecti hoc sentiamusLet us therefore as many as are perfect, be thus minded: and if you be any otherwise minded, this also God hath revealed to you. et si quid aliter sapitis et hoc vobis Deus revelabit
3:16 verumtamen ad quod pervenimus ut idem sapiamusNevertheless whereunto we are come, that we be of the same mind, let us continue in the same rule. et in eadem permaneamus regula
3:17 imitatores mei estote fratresBe followers of me, brethren, &; observe them that walk so as you have our form. et observate eos qui ita ambulant sicut habetis formam nos
3:18 multi enim ambulant quos saepe dicebam vobis nunc autem et flens dico inimicos crucis ChristiFor many walk whom often I told you of (and now weeping also I tell you) the enemies of the cross of Christ:
3:19 quorum finis interitusWhose end is destruction: whose God, is the belly: and their glory in their confusion, which mind worldly things. quorum deus venter et gloria in confusione ipsorum qui terrena sapiunt
4:1 itaque fratres mei carissimi et desiderantissimi gaudium meum et corona mea sic state in Domino carissimiTherefore, my dearest brethren and most desired, my joy and my crown: so stand in our Lord, my dearest.
4:2 Euhodiam rogoEuchodia I desire and Syntyche I beseech to be of one mind in our Lord. et Syntychen deprecor id ipsum sapere in Domino
4:3 etiam rogo et teYea and I beseech thee my sincere companion, help those women that have laboured with me in the Gospel with Clement, and the rest my coadjutors, whose names are in the book of life. germane conpar adiuva illas quae me cum laboraverunt in evangelio cum Clemente et ceteris adiutoribus meis quorum nomina sunt in libro vitae
4:4 gaudete in Domino semperRejoice in our Lord always; again I say rejoice. iterum dico gaudete
4:5 modestia vestra nota sit omnibus hominibusLet your modesty be known to all men. Our Lord is nigh. Dominus prope
4:6 nihil solliciti sitisBe nothing careful: but in every thing by prayer &; supplication with thanks-giving let your petitions be known with God. sed in omni oratione et obsecratione cum gratiarum actione petitiones vestrae innotescant apud Deum
4:7 et pax Dei quae exsuperat omnem sensum custodiat corda vestra et intellegentias vestras in Christo IesuAnd the peace of God which passeth all understanding, keep your hearts and intelligences in Christ Jesus.
4:8 de cetero fratres quaecumque sunt vera quaecumque pudica quaecumque iusta quaecumque sancta quaecumque amabilia quaecumque bonae famae si qua virtus si qua laus haec cogitateFor the rest, brethren, what things soever be true, whatsoever honest, whatsoever just, whatsoever holy, whatsoever amiable, whatsoever of good fame, if there be any virtue, if any praise of discipline, these things think upon.
4:9 quae et didicistis et accepistis et audistis et vidistis in me haec agiteWhich you have both learned, and received, and heard, &; seen in me; these things do ye, and the God of peace shall be with you. et Deus pacis erit vobis cum
4:10 gavisus sum autem in Domino vehementer quoniam tandem aliquando refloruistis pro me sentireAnd I rejoiced in our Lord exceedingly, that once at the length you have reflourished to care for me, as you did also care: but you were occupied. sicut et sentiebatis occupati autem eratis
4:11 non quasi propter penuriam dicoIspeak not as it were for penury. For I have learned, to be content with the things that I have. ego enim didici in quibus sum sufficiens esse
4:13 omnia possum in eo qui me confortatI can all things in him that strengtheneth me.
4:14 verumtamen bene fecistis communicantes tribulationi meaeNevertheless you have done well, communicating to my tribulation.
4:15 scitis autem et vos Philippenses quod in principio evangelii quando profectus sum a Macedonia nulla mihi ecclesia communicavit in ratione dati et accepti nisi vos soliAnd you also know, o Philippians, that in the beginning of the Gospel, when I departed from Macedonia, no Church communicated unto me in the account of gift and receit, but you only:
4:16 quia et Thessalonicam et semel et bis in usum mihi misistisFor unto Thessalonica also, once and twice you sent to my use.
4:17 non quia quaero datumNot that I seek the gift, but I seek the fruit abounding in your account. sed requiro fructum abundantem in rationem vestram
4:18 habeo autem omniaBut I have all things, and abound: I was filled after I received of Epaphroditus the things that you sent, an odour of sweetness, an acceptable host, pleasing God. et abundo repletus sum acceptis ab Epafrodito quae misistis odorem suavitatis hostiam acceptam placentem Deo
4:19 Deus autem meus impleat omne desiderium vestrum secundum divitias suas in gloria in Christo IesuAnd my God supply all your lack according to his riches in glory, in Christ Jesus.
4:20 Deo autem et Patri nostro gloria in saecula saeculorumAnd to God &; our Father be glory world without end. Amen. amen

4:22 salutant vos omnes sanctiThe brethren that are with me, salute you. All the saints salute you: but especially they that are of Cæsar';s house. maxime autem qui de Caesaris domo sunt
4:23 gratia Domini Iesu Christi cum spiritu vestroThe grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with your spirit. Amen. amen
