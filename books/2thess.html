1:1 Paulus et Silvanus et Timotheus ecclesiae Thessalonicensium in Deo Patre nostro et Domino Iesu ChristoPaul and Sylvanus and Timothee, to the church of the Thessalonians in God our Father and our Lord Jesus Christ.
1:2 gratia vobis et pax a Deo Patre nostro et Domino Iesu ChristoGrace to you and peace from God our Father and our Lord Jesus Christ.
1:3 gratias agere debemus Deo semper pro vobis fratres ita ut dignum estWe ought to give thanks always to God for you, brethren, so as meet is, because your faith increaseth exceedingly, and the charity of every one of you aboundeth towards each other: quoniam supercrescit fides vestra et abundat caritas uniuscuiusque omnium vestrum in invicem ita ut et nos ipsi in vobis gloriemur in ecclesiis Dei pro patientia vestra et fide in omnibus persecutionibus vestris et tribulationibus quas sustinetis in exemplum iusti iudicii Dei ut digni habeamini regno Dei pro quo et patimini si tamen iustum est apud Deum retribuere tribulationem his qui vos tribulant
1:7 et vobis qui tribulamini requiem nobis cum in revelatione Domini Iesu de caelo cum angelis virtutis eius in flamma ignis dantis vindictam his qui non noverunt Deumand to you that are vexed, rest with us in the revelation of our Lord Jesus from Heaven with the Angels of his power, et qui non oboediunt evangelio Domini nostri Iesu qui poenas dabunt in interitu aeternas a facie Domini et a gloria virtutis eius cum venerit glorificari in sanctis suis et admirabilis fieri in omnibus qui crediderunt quia creditum est testimonium nostrum super vos in die illo
1:11 in quo etiam oramus semper pro vobisWherein also we pray always for you, that our God make you worthy of his vocation, and accomplish all the good pleasure of his goodness &; the work of faith in power, ut dignetur vos vocatione sua Deus et impleat omnem voluntatem bonitatis et opus fidei in virtute ut clarificetur nomen Domini nostri Iesu Christi in vobis et vos in illo secundum gratiam Dei nostri et Domini Iesu Christi
2:1 rogamus autem vos fratres per adventum Domini nostri Iesu Christi et nostrae congregationis in ipsumAnd we desire you, brethren, by the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ, &; of our congregation into him; ut non cito moveamini a sensu ne que terreamini ne que per spiritum ne que per sermonem ne que per epistulam tamquam per nos quasi instet dies Domini
2:3 ne quis vos seducat ullo modo quoniamLet no man seduce you by any means, for unless there come a revolt first, and the man of sin be revealed, the son of perdition, nisi venerit discessio primum et revelatus fuerit homo peccati filius perditionis qui adversatur et extollitur supra omne quod dicitur Deus aut quod colitur ita ut in templo Dei sedeat ostendens se quia sit Deus
2:5 non retinetis quod cum adhuc essem apud vos haec dicebam vobisRemember you not, that when I was yet with you, I told you these things?
2:6 et nunc quid detineat scitisAnd now what letteth, you know: that he may be revealed in his time. ut reveletur in suo tempore
2:7 nam mysterium iam operatur iniquitatis(For now the mystery of iniquity worketh: only that he which now holdeth, do hold, until he be taken out of the way.) tantum ut qui tenet nunc donec de medio fiat
2:8 et tunc revelabitur ille iniquus quem Dominus Iesus interficiet spiritu oris suiAnd then that wicked one shall be revealed whom our Lord Jesus shall kill with the spirit of his mouth; and shall destroy with the manifestation of his advent, him, et destruet inlustratione adventus sui
2:11 ideo mittit illis Deus operationem errorisTherefore God will send them the operation of error, to believe lying: ut credant mendacio ut iudicentur omnes qui non crediderunt veritati sed consenserunt iniquitati
2:13 nos autem debemus gratias agere Deo semper pro vobis fratres dilecti a DeoBut we ought to give thanks to God always for you, brethren beloved of God, that he hath chosen you first-fruits unto salvation, in sanctification of spirit and faith of the truth: quod elegerit nos Deus primitias in salutem in sanctificatione Spiritus et fide veritatis
2:14 ad quod et vocavit vos per evangelium nostrum in adquisitionem gloriae Domini nostri Iesu Christiinto the which also he hath called you by our Gospel, unto the purchasing of the glory of our Lord Jesus Christ.
2:15 itaque fratres stateTherefore, brethren, stand; and hold the traditions which you have learned, whether it be by word, or by our epistle. et tenete traditiones quas didicistis sive per sermonem sive per epistulam nostram
2:16 ipse autem Dominus noster Iesus ChristusAnd our Lord Jesus Christ himself, and God and our Father which hath loved us, and hath given eternal consolation, and good hope in grace, et Deus et Pater noster qui dilexit nos et dedit consolationem aeternam et spem bonam in gratia exhortetur corda vestra et confirmet in omni opere et sermone bono
3:1 de cetero fratres orate pro nobisFor the rest, brethren, pray for us, that the word of God may have course and be glorified, as also with you: ut sermo Domini currat et clarificetur sicut et apud vos et ut liberemur ab inportunis et malis hominibus
3:2 non enim omnium est fidesand that we may be delivered from importunate and naughty men. For all men have not faith.
3:3 fidelis autem Dominus est qui confirmabit vosBut our Lord is faithful, who will confirm and keep you from evil. et custodiet a malo
3:4 confidimus autem de vobis in Domino quoniam quae praecipimus et facitisAnd we have confidence of you in our Lord, that the things which we command, both you do, and will do. et facietis
3:5 Dominus autem dirigat corda vestra in caritate Dei et patientia ChristiAnd our Lord direct your hearts in the charity of God, and patience of Christ.
3:6 denuntiamus autem vobis fratres in nomine Domini nostri Iesu Christi ut subtrahatis vos ab omni fratre ambulante inordinateAnd we denounce unto you, brethren, in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ, that you withdraw yourselves from every brother walking inordinately, and not according to the tradition which they have received of us. et non secundum traditionem quam acceperunt a nobis
3:7 ipsi enim scitis quemadmodum oporteat imitari nosFor yourselves know how you ought to imitate us: for we have not been unquiet among you: quoniam non inquieti fuimus inter vos ne que gratis panem manducavimus ab aliquo sed in labore et fatigatione nocte et die operantes ne quem vestrum gravaremus
3:9 non quasi non habuerimus potestatemNot as though we had not authority: but that we might give ourselves a pattern unto you for to imitate us. sed ut nos met ipsos formam daremus vobis ad imitandum nos
3:10 nam et cum essemus apud vos hoc denuntiabamus vobis quoniam si quis non vult operari ne c manducetFor also when we were with you, this we denounced to you, that if any will not work, neither let him eat.
3:11 audimus enim inter vos quosdam ambulare inquiete nihil operantes sed curiose agentesFor we have heard of certain among you that walk unquietly, working nothing, but curiously meddling.
3:12 his autem qui eius modi sunt denuntiamusAnd to them that be such we denounce, &; beseech them in our Lord Jesus Christ, that working with silence, they eat their own bread. et obsecramus in Domino Iesu Christo ut cum silentio operantes suum panem manducent
3:13 vos autem fratres nolite deficere benefacientesBut you, brethren faint not well-doing.
3:14 quod si quis non oboedit verbo nostro per epistulam hunc notateAnd if any obey not our word, note him by an epistle: et non commisceamini cum illo ut confundatur
3:15 et nolite quasi inimicum existimareand do not company with him, that he may be confounded: and do not esteem him as an enemy, but admonish him as a brother. sed corripite ut fratrem
3:16 ipse autem Dominus pacis det vobis pacem sempiternam in omni locoAnd the Lord of peace himself give you everlasting peace in every place. Our Lord be with you all. Dominus cum omnibus vobis
3:17 salutatio mea manu Pauli quod est signum in omni epistulaThe salutation, with mine own hand, Paul';s: which is a sign in every epistle. So I write. ita scribo
3:18 gratia Domini nostri Iesu Christi cum omnibus vobisThe grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with you all. Amen.
