1:1 Paulus apostolus Iesu Christi per voluntatem Dei et Timotheus frater ecclesiae Dei quae est Corinthi cum sanctis omnibus qui sunt in universa AchaiaPaul an Apostle of Jesus Christ by the will of God, and Timothee our brother: to the Church of God that is at Corinth, with all the saints that are in all Achaia.
1:2 gratia vobis et pax a Deo Patre nostro et Domino Iesu ChristoGrace unto you and peace from God our Father, &; from our Lord Jesus Christ.
1:3 benedictus Deus et Pater Domini nostri Iesu Christi Pater misericordiarum et Deus totius consolationis qui consolatur nos in omni tribulatione nostraBlessed be the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, the Father of mercies and God of all comfort, who comforteth us in all our tribulation; that we also may be able to comfort them that are in all distress, by the exhortation wherewith we also are exhorted of God. ut possimus et ipsi consolari eos qui in omni pressura sunt per exhortationem qua exhortamur et ipsi a DeoFor as the passions of Christ abound in us: quoniam sicut abundant passiones Christi in nobis ita et per Christum abundat consolatio nostraso also by Christ doth our comfort abound.
1:6 si ve autem tribulamur pro vestra exhortatione et saluteAnd whether we be in tribulation, for your exhortation &; salvation: si ve exhortamur pro vestra exhortatione quae operatur in tolerantia earundem passionum quas et nos patimurwhether we be exhorted, for your exhortation &; salvation, which worketh the toleration of the same passions which we also do suffer:
1:7 et spes nostra firma pro vobisand our hope is firm for you: scientes quoniam sicut socii passionum estis sic eritis et consolationisknowing that as you are partakers of the passions, so shall you be of the consolation also.
1:8 non enim volumus ignorare vos fratres de tribulatione nostra quae facta est in Asia quoniam supra modum gravati sumus supra virtutem itaFor we will not have you ignorant, brethren, concerning our tribulation, which happened in Asia, that we were pressed above measure above our power, ut taederet nos etiam vivereso that it was tedious unto us even to live.
1:9 sed ipsi in nobis ipsis responsum mortis habuimus ut non simus fidentes in nobis sed in Deo qui suscitat mortuosBut we in ourselves, had the answer of death, that we be not trusting in ourselves, but in God who raiseth up the dead,
1:10 qui de tantis periculis eripuit noswho hath delivered and doth deliver us out of so great dangers: et eruetin whom we hope that he will yet also deliver us, in quem speramus quoniam et adhuc eripiet adiuvantibus et vobis in oratione pro nobis
1:11 ut ex multis personis eius quae in nobis est donationis per multos gratiae agantur pro nobisyou helping withal in prayer for us, that by many men';s persons, thanks for that gift which is in us, may be given by many in our behalf.
1:12 nam gloria nostra haec estFor our glory is this, the testimony of our conscience, that in simplicity and sincerity of God, and not in carnal wisdom, but in the grace of God we have conversed in this world: testimonium conscientiae nostrae quod in simplicitate et sinceritate Dei et non in sapientia carnali sed in gratia Dei conversati sumus in mundo abundantius autem ad vosand more aboundantly towards you.
1:13 non enim alia scribimus vobis quam quae legistis et cognoscitisFor we write no other things to you than that you have read and know. spero autem quod usque in finem cognoscetisAnd I hope that you shall know unto the end: sicut et cognovistis nos ex parte quia gloria vestra sumus sicut et vos nostra in die Domini nostri Iesu Christias also you have known us in part, that we are your glory, as you also ours in the day of our Lord Jesus Christ.
1:15 et hac confidentia volui prius venire ad vosAnd in this confidence I would first have come to you, ut secundam gratiam haberetisthat you might have a second grace: et per vos transire in Macedoniam et iterum a Macedonia venire ad vos et a vobis deduci in Iudaeamand by you pass into Macedonia, and again from Macedonia come to you, and of you be brought on my way into Jewry.

1:18 fidelis autem Deus quia sermo noster qui fit apud vos non est in illo est et nonBut God is faithful, because our preaching which was to you, there is not in it, It is, and, It is not.
1:19 Dei enim Filius Iesus Christus qui in vobis per nos praedicatus est per me et Silvanum et Timotheum non fuit est et nonFor the Son of God Jesus Christ, who by us was preached among you, by me and Sylvanus and Timothee, was not, It is, and, It is not, but, sed est in illo fuitIt is, was in him.

1:22 et qui signavit noswho also hath sealed us, et dedit pignus Spiritus in cordibus nostrisand given the pledge of the Spirit in our hearts.
1:23 ego autem testem Deum invoco in animam meam quod parcens vobis non veni ultra CorinthumAnd I call God to witness upon my soul, that sparing you, I came not any more to Corinth, not because we over-rule your faith: but, we are helpers of your joy. For in the faith you stand.

For we are the good odour of Christ unto God in them that are saved, &; in them that perish.

3:1 incipimus iterum nos met ipsos commendareBegin we again to commend our-selves? or do we need (as certain) epistles of commendation to you, aut numquid egemus sicut quidam commendaticiis epistulis ad vos aut ex vobisof from you?
3:2 epistula nostra vos estis scripta in cordibus nostris quae scitur et legitur ab omnibus hominibus manifestati quoniam epistula estis Christi ministrata a nobis et scripta non atramento sed Spiritu Dei vivi non in tabulis lapideis sed in tabulis cordis carnalibusOur epistle you are, written in our hearts, which is known &; read of all men: being manifested that you are the epistle of Christ, ministered by us, &; written not with ink, but with the Spirit of the living God: not in tables of stone, but in the tables carnal of the heart.
3:4 fiduciam autem talem habemus per Christum ad DeumAnd such confidence we have by Christ to God:
3:5 non quod sufficientes simus cogitare aliquid a nobis quasi ex nobisnot that we be sufficient to think any thing of our-selves, as of our-selves: sed sufficientia nostra ex Deo est qui et idoneos nos fecit ministros novi testamenti non litterae sed Spiritusbut our sufficiency is of God.
3:6 littera enim occiditWho also hath made us meet ministers of the new testament: not in the letter, but in the Spirit. Spiritus autem vivificatFor the letter killeth: but the Spirit quickeneth.
3:7 quod si ministratio mortis litteris deformata in lapidibus fuit in gloria itaAnd if the ministration of death with letters figured in stones, was in glory, ut non possent intendere filii Israhel in faciem Mosi propter gloriam vultus eius quae evacuaturso that the children of Israel could not behold the face of Moyses for the glory of his countenance, that is made void; quomodo non magis ministratio Spiritus erit in gloriahow shall not the ministration of the Spirit be more in glory?
3:9 nam si ministratio damnationis gloria est multo magis abundat ministerium iustitiae in gloriaFor if the ministration of damnation be in glory, much more the ministry of justice aboundeth in glory.
3:10 nam nec glorificatum est quod claruit in hac parte propter excellentem gloriamFor neither was it glorified, which in this part was glorious, by reason of the excelling glory.
3:11 si enim quod evacuatur per gloriam est multo magis quod manet in gloria estFor if that which is made void, is by glory: much more that which abideth, is in glory.
3:12 habentes igitur talem spem multa fiducia utimurHaving therefore such hope, et non sicut Moses ponebat velamen super faciem suamwe use much confidence: and not as Moyses put a veil upon his face, ut non intenderent filii Israhel in faciem eius quod evacuaturthat the children of Israel might not behold his face, which is made void.
3:14 sed obtusi sunt sensus eorumbut their senses were dulled. usque in hodiernum enim diem id ipsum velamen in lectione veteris testamenti manet non revelatumFor until this present day, quoniam in Christo evacuaturthe self-same veil in the lecture of the old testament remaineth unrevealed (because in Christ it is made void) but until this present day, sed usque in hodiernum diem cum legitur Moses velamen est positum super cor eorumwhen Moyses is read, a veil is put upon their heart.
3:16 cum autem conversus fuerit ad Deum aufertur velamenBut when he shall be converted to our Lord, the veil shall be taken away.
3:17 Dominus autem Spiritus estAnd our Lord is a Spirit. ubi autem Spiritus Domini ibi libertasAnd where the Spirit of our Lord is, there is liberty.
3:18 nos vero omnes revelata facie gloriam Domini speculantes in eandem imaginem transformamurBut we all, beholding the glory of our Lord with face revealed, a claritate in claritatem tamquam a Domini Spirituare transformed into the same image from glory unto glory, as of our Lord';s Spirit.

13:4 nam etsi crucifixus est ex infirmitate sed vivit ex virtute DeiFor although he was crucified of infirmity; yet he liveth by the power of God. nam et nos infirmi sumus in illoFor we also are weak in him: sed vivemus cum eo ex virtute Dei in vobisbut we shall live with him by the power of God on you.
13:5 vos met ipsos temptateTry your own selves if you be in the faith, si estis in fide ipsi vos probateprove ye yourselves. an non cognoscitis vos ipsos quia Christus Iesus in vobis estKnow you not yourselves that Christ Jesus is in you, nisi forte reprobi estisunless perhaps you be reprobates.
13:6 spero autem quod cognoscetis quia nos non sumus reprobiBut I hope you know that we are not reprobates.
13:7 oramus autem Deum ut nihil mali faciatisAnd we pray God, that you do no evil, non ut nos probati pareamusnot that we may appear approved, sed ut vos quod bonum est faciatisbut that you may do that which is good, nos autem ut reprobi simusand we be as reprobates.
13:8 non enim possumus aliquid adversus veritatem sed pro veritateFor we cannot any thing against the truth; but for the truth.
13:9 gaudemus enim quando nos infirmi sumusFor we rejoice, for that we are weak, vos autem potentes estisand you are mighty. hoc et oramus vestram consummationemThis also we pray for, your consummation.
13:10 ideo haec absens scriboTherefore these things I write absent: ut non praesens durius agam secundum potestatem quam Dominus dedit mihi in aedificationem et non in destructionemthat being present I may not deal hardly according to the power which our Lord hath given me unto edification and not unto destruction.
13:11 de cetero fratres gaudeteFor the rest, brethren, perfecti estoterejoice, exhortaminibe perfect, idem sapitetake exhortation, pacem habetebe of one mind, et Deus dilectionis et pacis erit vobis cumhave peace; and the God of peace &; love shall be with you.
13:12 salutate invicem in osculo sanctoSalute one another in a holy kiss. salutant vos sancti omnesAll the saints salute you.
13:13 gratia Domini nostri Iesu Christi et caritas Dei et communicatio Sancti Spiritus cum omnibus vobisThe grace of our Lord Jesus Christ, and the charity of God, and the communication of the Holy Ghost be with you all. amenAmen.
