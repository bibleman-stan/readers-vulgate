1:1 Paulus et Silvanus et Timotheus ecclesiae Thessalonicensium in Deo Patre et Domino Iesu ChristoPaul and Sylvanus and Timothee to the Church of the Thessalonians in God the Father, and our Lord Jesus Christ. Grace to you and peace. gratia vobis et pax
1:2 gratias agimus Deo semper pro omnibus vobis memoriam facientes in orationibus nostris sine intermissione memores operis fidei vestrae et laboris et caritatis et sustinentiae spei Domini nostri Iesu Christi ante Deum et Patrem nostrumWe give thanks to God always for all you; making a memory of you in prayers without intermission,
1:4 scientes fratres dilecti a Deo electionem vestramknowing, brethren beloved of God, your election:
1:5 quia evangelium nostrum non fuit ad vos in sermone tantum sed et in virtute et in Spiritu Sancto et in plenitudine multathat our Gospel hath not been to you in word only, but in power &; the Holy Ghost, &; in much fullness, as you know what manner of men we have been among you for your sakes. sicut scitis quales fuerimus vobis propter vos
1:6 et vos imitatores nostri facti estis et Domini excipientes verbum in tribulatione multa cum gaudio Spiritus SanctiAnd you became followers of us, &; of our Lord; receiving the word in much tribulation, with joy of the Holy Ghost:
1:7 itaso that you were made a pattern to all that believe in Macedonia &; in Achaia. ut facti sitis forma omnibus credentibus in Macedonia et in Achaia
1:8 a vobis enim diffamatus est sermo Domini non solum in Macedonia et in AchaiaFor from you was bruited the word of our Lord: not only in Macedonia and in Achaia, but also in every place, your faith which is to God-ward, is proceeded, so that it is not necessary for us to speak any thing. sed in omni loco fides vestra quae est ad Deum profecta est ita ut non sit nobis necesse quicquam loqui
1:9 ipsi enim de nobis adnuntiant qualem introitum habuerimus ad vosFor they themselves report of us what manner of entering we had to you; and how you are turned to God from idols, to serve the living and true God, et quomodo conversi estis ad Deum a simulacris servire Deo vivo et vero et expectare Filium eius de caelis quem suscitavit ex mortuis Iesum qui eripuit nos ab ira ventura
2:1 nam ipsi scitis fratres introitum nostrum ad vos quia non inanis fuitFor yourselves know, brethren, our entrance unto you, that it was not vain:
2:2 sed ante passi et contumeliis affectibut having suffered before and been abused with contumelies (as you know) at Philippi, we had confidence in our God, to speak unto you the Gospel of God in much carefulness. sicut scitis in Philippis fiduciam habuimus in Deo nostro loqui ad vos evangelium Dei in multa sollicitudine
2:3 exhortatio enim nostra non de errore ne que de inmunditia ne que in doloFor our exhortation was not of error, nor of uncleanness, nor in deceit: sed sicut probati sumus a Deo ut crederetur nobis evangelium
2:4 ita loquimur non quasi hominibus placentesbut as we were approved of God that the Gospel should be committed to us, so we speak: not as pleasing men, but God, who proveth our hearts. sed Deo qui probat corda nostra
2:5 ne que enim aliquando fuimus in sermone adulationisFor neither have we been, at any time in the word of adulation, as you know; nor in occasion of avarice, God is witness: sicut scitis ne que in occasione avaritiae Deus testis est
2:7 sed facti sumus lenes in medio vestrumWhereas we might have been a burden to you, as the Apostles of Christ; but we became children in the midst of you, as if a nurse should cherish her children: tamquam si nutrix foveat filios suos
2:8 ita desiderantes vos cupide volebamus tradere vobis non solum evangelium Dei sed etiam animas nostras quoniam carissimi nobis facti estisso having a desire to you, we would gladly deliver unto you not only the Gospel of God, but also our own souls because you are become most dear unto us.
2:9 memores enim estis fratres laborem nostrum et fatigationemFor you are mindful, brethren, of our labour and toil. Day and night working, lest we should charge any of you, we preached among you the Gospel of God. nocte et die operantes ne quem vestrum gravaremus praedicavimus in vobis evangelium Dei
2:10 vos testes estis et Deus quam sancte et iuste et sine querella vobis qui credidistis fuimusYou are witnesses and God, how holily, and justly &; without blame, we have been to you that did believe.
2:11 sicut scitisAs you know in what manner we desiring and comforting you, have adjured every one of you (as a father his children) qualiter unumquemque vestrum tamquam pater filios suos deprecantes vos et consolantes testificati sumus ut ambularetis digne Deo qui vocavit vos in suum regnum et gloriam
2:14 vos enim imitatores facti estis fratres ecclesiarum Dei quae sunt in Iudaea in Christo IesuFor you, brethren, are become followers of the Churches of God that be in Jewry, in Christ Jesus: for you also have suffered the same things of your own lineage, as they also of the Jews, quia eadem passi estis et vos a contribulibus vestris sicut et ipsi a Iudaeis qui et Dominum occiderunt Iesum et prophetas et nos persecuti sunt et Deo non placent et omnibus hominibus adversantur prohibentes nos gentibus loqui ut salvae fiant ut impleant peccata sua semper
2:16 praevenit autem ira Dei super illos usque in finemprohibiting us to speak to the Gentiles that they may be saved, to make up their sins always. For the wrath of God is come upon them even to the end.
2:17 nos autem fratres desolati a vobis ad tempus horae aspectu non corde abundantius festinavimus faciem vestram videre cum multo desiderioBut we, brethren, deprived of you for a short time, in sight, not in heart; have hastened the more aboundantly to see your face with much desire.
2:18 quoniam voluimus venire ad vos ego quidem Paulus et semel et iterumFor we would have come to you, I Paul certes, once and again: but Satan hath hindered us. et inpedivit nos Satanas
2:19 quae est enim nostra spes aut gaudium aut corona gloriaeFor what is our hope, or joy, or crown of glory? Are not you, before our Lord Jesus Christ in his coming? nonne vos ante Dominum nostrum Iesum estis in adventu eius
2:20 vos enim estis gloria nostra et gaudiumFor you are our glory and joy.
3:1 propter quod non sustinentes amplius placuit nobis remanere Athenis solisFor the which cause forbearing no longer, it pleased us to remain at Athens, alone.
3:2 et misimus Timotheum fratrem nostrum et ministrum Dei in evangelio Christi ad confirmandos vos et exhortandos pro fide vestraAnd we sent Timothee our brother, &; the minister of God in the Gospel of Christ, to confirm you and exhort you for your faith, ut nemo moveatur in tribulationibus istis
3:3 ipsi enim scitis quod in hoc positi sumusthat no man be moved in these tribulations: for yourselves know, that we are appointed to this.
3:4 nam et cum apud vos essemus praedicebamus vobis passuros nos tribulationesFor even when we were with you, we foretold you that we should suffer tribulations, as also it is come to pass, and you know. sicut et factum est et scitis
3:5 propterea et ego amplius non sustinens misi ad cognoscendam fidem vestram ne forte temptaverit vos is qui temptatTherefore I also forbearing no longer, sent to know your faith: lest perhaps he that tempteth, hath tempted you, and our labour be made vain. et inanis fiat labor noster
3:6 nunc autem veniente Timotheo ad nos a vobisBut now Timothee coming unto us from you, and reporting to us your faith and charity, and that you have a good remembrance of us always, desiring to see us, as we also you: et adnuntiante nobis fidem et caritatem vestram et quia memoriam nostri habetis bonam semper desiderantes nos videre sicut nos quoque vos
3:7 ideo consolati sumus fratres in vobis in omni necessitate et tribulatione nostra per vestram fidemtherefore we are comforted, brethren, in you, in all our necessity, &; tribulation, by your faith, quoniam nunc vivimus si vos statis in Domino
3:9 quam enim gratiarum actionem possumus Deo retribuere pro vobis in omni gaudio quo gaudemus propter vos ante Deum nostrum nocte et die abundantius orantes ut videamus faciem vestramFor what thanks-giving can we render to God for you, in all joy wherewith we rejoice for you before our God, et conpleamus ea quae desunt fidei vestrae
3:11 ipse autem Deus et Pater noster et Dominus Iesus dirigat viam nostram ad vosAnd God himself and our Father, &; our Lord Jesus Christ direct our way to you.
3:13 amento confirm your hearts without blame, in holiness, before God and our Father, in the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ with all his Saints. Amen.
4:1 de cetero ergo fratres rogamus vosFor the rest therefore, brethren, we desire and beseech you in our Lord Jesus, that as you have received of us how you ought to walk, and to please God, as also you do walk, that you abound more. et obsecramus in Domino Iesu ut quemadmodum accepistis a nobis quomodo vos oporteat ambulare et placere Deo sicut et ambulatis ut abundetis magis
4:2 scitis enim quae praecepta dederimus vobis per Dominum IesumFor you know what precepts I have given to you by our Lord Jesus.
4:3 haec est enim voluntas Dei sanctificatio vestraFor this is the will of God, your sanctification: that you abstain from fornication,
4:4 ut abstineatis vos a fornicatione ut sciat unusquisque vestrum suum vas possidere in sanctificatione et honorethat every one may know to possess his vessel in sanctification and honour: non in passione desiderii sicut et gentes quae ignorant Deum
4:6 ut ne quis supergrediaturand that no man over-go, nor circumvent his brother in business: because our Lord is revenger of all these things, as we have foretold you, and have testified. ne que circumveniat in negotio fratrem suum quoniam vindex est Dominus de his omnibus sicut et praediximus vobis et testificati sumus
4:7 non enim vocavit nos Deus in inmunditia sed in sanctificationeFor God hath not called us into uncleanness, but into sanctification.
4:8 itaque qui spernit non hominem spernitTherefore he that despiseth these things, despiseth not man but God, who also hath given his holy Spirit in us. sed Deum qui etiam dedit Spiritum suum Sanctum in vobis
4:9 de caritate autem fraternitatis non necesse habemus scribere vobisBut concerning the charity of the Fraternity, we have no need to write to you: For yourselves have learned of God to love one another. ipsi enim vos a Deo didicistis ut diligatis invicem
4:10 etenim facitis illud in omnes fratres in universa MacedoniaYea and you do it toward all the brethren in all Macedonia. But we desire you, brethren, that you abound more: rogamus autem vos fratres ut abundetis magis et operam detis ut quieti sitis et ut vestrum negotium agatis et operemini manibus vestris sicut praecepimus vobis et ut honeste ambuletis ad eos qui foris sunt et nullius aliquid desideretis
4:13 nolumus autem vos ignorare fratres de dormientibusAnd we will not have you ignorant, brethren, concerning them that sleep, that you be not sorrowful, as also others that have no hope. ut non contristemini sicut et ceteri qui spem non habent
4:14 si enim credimus quod Iesus mortuus estFor if we believe that Jesus died and rose again, so also God them that have slept by Jesus will bring with him. et resurrexit ita et Deus eos qui dormierunt per Iesum adducet cum eo
4:15 hoc enim vobis dicimus in verbo Domini quia nos qui vivimusFor this we say to you in the word of our Lord, that we which live, which are remaining in the advent of our Lord, shall not prevent them that have slept. qui residui sumus in adventum Domini non praeveniemus eos qui dormierunt
4:16 quoniam ipse Dominus in iussu et in voce archangeli et in tuba Dei descendet de caeloFor our Lord himself in commandment, and in the voice of an Archangel, &; in the trumpet of God will descend from heaven: and the dead that are in Christ, shall rise again first. et mortui qui in Christo sunt resurgent primi
4:17 deinde nos qui vivimus qui relinquimur simul rapiemur cum illis in nubibus obviam Domino in aeraThen we that live, that are left, withal shall be taken up with them in the clouds to meet Christ, into the air, and so always we shall be with our Lord. et sic semper cum Domino erimus
4:18 itaque consolamini invicem in verbis istisTherefore comfort ye one another in these words.
5:1 de temporibus autem et momentis fratres non indigetis ut scribamus vobisAnd of the times and moments, brethren, you need not that we write to you.
5:2 ipsi enim diligenter scitis quia dies Domini sicut fur in nocte ita venietFor yourselves know perfectly that the day of our Lord shall so come, as a thief in the night.
5:3 cum enim dixerintFor when they shall say, peace &; security; then shall sudden destruction come upon them, as the pains to her that is with child, and they shall not escape. pax et securitas tunc repentinus eis superveniet interitus sicut dolor in utero habenti et non effugient
5:4 vos autem fratres non estis in tenebrisBut you, brethren, are not in darkness; that the same day may as a thief over-take you. ut vos dies ille tamquam fur conprehendat
5:5 omnes enim vos filii lucis estis et filii dieiFor all you are the children of light, and children of the day: we are not of the night nor of darkness. non sumus noctis ne que tenebrarum
5:6 igitur non dormiamus sicut ceteriTherefore let us not sleep as also others: but let us watch &; be sober. sed vigilemus et sobrii simus
5:7 qui enim dormiunt nocte dormiuntFor they that sleep, sleep in the night; &; they that be drunk, be drunk in the night. et qui ebrii sunt nocte ebrii sunt
5:8 nos autem qui diei sumus sobrii simus induti loricam fidei et caritatis et galeam spem salutisBut we that are of the day, are sober, having on, the breast-plate of faith and charity, and a helmet, the hope of salvation.
5:9 quoniam non posuit nos Deus in iram sed in adquisitionem salutis per Dominum nostrum Iesum Christum qui mortuus est pro nobisFor God hath not appointed us unto wrath, but unto the purchasing of salvation by our Lord Jesus Christ, ut si ve vigilemus si ve dormiamus simul cum illo vivamus
5:11 propter quod consolamini invicemFor the which cause comfort one another: and edify one another, as also you do. et aedificate alterutrum sicut et facitis
5:12 rogamus autem vos fratres ut noveritis eos qui laborant inter vosAnd we beseech you, brethren, that you will know them that labour among you, and that govern you in our Lord and admonish you: et praesunt vobis in Domino et monent vos ut habeatis illos abundantius in caritate propter opus illorum
5:13 pacem habete cum eisthat you have them more aboundantly in charity for their work. Have peace with them.
5:14 rogamus autem vosAnd we beseech you, brethren, admonish the unquiet, comfort the weak-minded, bear up the weak, be patient to all. fratres corripite inquietos consolamini pusillianimes suscipite infirmos patientes estote ad omnes
5:15 videte ne quis malum pro malo alicui reddatSee that none render evil for evil to any man: but always that which is good pursue towards each other, &; towards all. sed semper quod bonum est sectamini et in invicem et in omnes
5:16 semper gaudeteAlways rejoice.
5:17 sine intermissione oratePray without intermission.
5:18 in omnibus gratias agiteIn all things give thanks. For this is the will of God in Christ Jesus in all you. haec enim voluntas Dei est in Christo Iesu in omnibus vobis
5:19 Spiritum nolite extinguereThe Spirit extinguish not.
5:20 prophetias nolite spernereProphecies despise not.
5:21 omnia autem probateBut prove all things: hold that which is good. quod bonum est tenete
5:22 ab omni specie mala abstinete vosFrom all appearance of evil refrain yourselves.
5:23 ipse autem Deus pacis sanctificet vos per omniaAnd the God of peace himself sanctify you in all things: that your whole spirit, and soul and body without blame may be preserved in the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ. et integer spiritus vester et anima et corpus sine querella in adventu Domini nostri Iesu Christi servetur
5:24 fidelis est qui vocavit vosHe is faithful, that hath called you, who also will do it. qui etiam faciet
5:25 fratres orate pro nobisBrethren pray for us.
5:26 salutate fratres omnes in osculo sanctoSalute all the brethren in a holy kiss.
5:27 adiuro vos per Dominum ut legatur epistula omnibus sanctis fratribusI adjure you by our Lord that this epistle be read to all the holy brethren.
5:28 gratia Domini nostri Iesu Christi vobis cumThe grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with you. Amen. amen
