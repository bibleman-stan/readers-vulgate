1:1 Paulus apostolus non ab hominibus ne que per hominem sed per Iesum Christum et Deum Patrem qui suscitavit eum a mortuisPaul an Apostle not of men, neither by man, but by Jesus Christ, and God the Father that raised him from the dead, et qui me cum sunt omnes fratresand all the brethren that are with me; ecclesiis Galatiaeto the churches of Galatia.
1:3 gratia vobis et pax a Deo Patre et Domino nostro Iesu Christo qui dedit se met ipsum pro peccatis nostrisGrace to you and peace from God the Father and our Lord Jesus Christ, who gave himself for our sins, ut eriperet nos de praesenti saeculo nequam secundum voluntatem Dei et Patris nostri cui est gloria in saecula saeculorumthat he might deliver us from this present wicked world, according to the will of our God and Father:
1:5 amento whom is glory for ever and ever. Amen.
1:6 miror quod sic tam cito transferimini ab eo qui vos vocavit in gratiam Christi in aliud evangelium quod non est aliud nisi sunt aliqui qui vos conturbant et volunt convertere evangelium ChristiI marvel that thus so soon you are transferred from him that called you into the grace of Christ, unto another Gospel: which is not another, unless there be some that trouble you, and will invert the Gospel of Christ.
1:8 sed licet nos aut angelus de caelo evangelizet vobisBut although we, or an Angel from Heaven, praeterquam quod evangelizavimus vobisevangelize to you beside that which we have evangelized to you, anathema sitbe he anathema.
1:9 sicut praediximusAs we have said before, et nunc iterum dicoso now I say again: si quis vobis evangelizaverit praeter id quod accepistis anathema sitIf any evangelize to you, beside that which you have received, be he anathema.
1:10 modo enim hominibus suadeo aut DeoFor do I now use persuasion to men, aut quaero hominibus placereor to God? Or do I seek to please men? If I yet did please men, si adhuc hominibus placerem Christi servus non essemI should not be the servant of Christ.
1:11 notum enim vobis facio fratres evangelium quod evangelizatum est a me quia non est secundum hominemFor I do you to understand, brethren, the Gospel that was evangelized of me, that it is not according to man.
1:12 ne que enim ego ab homine accepi illudFor neither did I receive it of man, nor learn it; ne que didicibut by the revelation of Jesus Christ. sed per revelationem Iesu ChristiFor you have heard my conversation sometime in Judaism, that above measure I persecuted the Church of God, and expugned it, and profited in Judaism above many of mine equals in my nation, being more abundantly an emulator of the traditions of my fathers.
1:15 cum autem placuit ei qui me segregavit de utero matris meae et vocavit per gratiam suamBut when it pleased him that separated me from my mother';s womb, and called me by his grace, ut revelaret Filium suum in meto reveal his Son in me, ut evangelizarem illum in gentibusthat I should evangelize him among the Gentiles, continuo non adquievi carni et sanguiniincontinent I condescended not to flesh and blood, ne que veni Hierosolyma ad antecessores meos apostolosneither came I to Jerusalem to the Apostles my antecessors: sed abii in Arabiambut I went into Arabia, et iterum reversus sum Damascumand again I returned to Damascus.
1:18 deinde post annos tres veni Hierosolyma videre PetrumThen, after three years I came to Jerusalem to see Peter: et mansi apud eum diebus quindecimand tarried with him fifteen days.
1:19 alium autem apostolorum vidi neminem nisi Iacobum fratrem DominiBut other of the Apostles saw I none; saving James the brother of our Lord.
1:20 quae autem scribo vobis ecce coram Deo quia non mentiorAnd the things that I write to you; behold before God, that I lie not.
1:21 deinde veni in partes Syriae et CiliciaeAfter that, I came into the parts of Syria and Cilicia.
1:22 eram autem ignotus facie ecclesiis Iudaeae quae erant in ChristoAnd I was unknown by sight to the churches of Jewry, that were in Christ:
1:23 tantum autem auditum habebant quoniambut they had heard only, qui persequebatur nos aliquando nunc evangelizat fidem quam aliquando expugnabatThat he which persecuted us sometime, doth now evangelize the faith which sometime he expugned:
1:24 et in me clarificabant Deumand in me they glorified God.
2:1 deinde post annos quattuordecim iterum ascendi Hierosolyma cum Barnaba adsumpto et TitoThen after fourteen years I went up again to Jerusalem with Barnabas, taking Titus also with me.
2:2 ascendi autem secundum revelationemAnd I went up according to revelation: et contuli cum illis evangelium quod praedico in gentibusand conferred with them the Gospel which I preach among the Gentiles, but apart with them that seemed to be something, lest perhaps in vain I should run or had run.
2:3 sed neque Titus qui me cum erat cum esset gentilis conpulsus est circumcidiBut neither Titus which was with me, whereas he was a Gentile, was compelled to be circumcised:
2:4 sed propter subintroductos falsos fratres qui subintroierunt explorare libertatem nostram quam habemus in Christo Iesu ut nos in servitutem redigerentbut because of the false brethren craftily brought in, which craftily came in to espy our liberty that we have in Christ Jesus, that they might bring us into servitude. To whom we yielded not subjection no not for an hour, that the truth of the Gospel may remain with you.
2:6 ab iis autem qui videbantur esse aliquidBut of them that seemed to be something, (what they were some-time, it is nothing to me. quales aliquando fuerint nihil mea interestGod accepteth not the person of man) for to me, they that seemed to be something, added nothing. But contrarywise when they had seen, that to me was committed the Gospel of the prepuce, as to Peter of the circumcision (for he that wrought in Peter to the Apostleship of the circumcision, wrought in me also among the Gentiles) and when they had known the grace that was given me, James and Cephas and John, which seemed to be pillars, gave to me and Barnabas the right hands of society: Deus personam hominis non accipitthat we unto the Gentiles, &; they unto the circumcision: mihi enim qui videbantur nihil contuleruntonly that we should be mindful of the poor: the which same thing also I was careful to do.
2:11 cum autem venisset Cephas Antiochiam in faciem ei restiti quia reprehensibilis eratAnd when Cephas was come to Antioch, I resisted him in face, because he was reprehensible.
2:12 prius enim quam venirent quidam ab Iacobo cum gentibus edebatFor before that certain came from James, he did eat with the Gentiles: cum autem venissent subtrahebatbut when they were come, et segregabat se timens eos qui ex circumcisione eranthe withdrew and separated himself, fearing them that were of the circumcision.
2:13 et simulationi eius consenserunt ceteri Iudaei itaAnd to his simulation consented the rest of the Jews, ut et Barnabas duceretur ab eis in illa simulationeso that Barnabas also was led of them into that simulation.
2:14 sed cum vidissem quod non recte ambularent ad veritatem evangelii dixi Cephae coram omnibusBut when I saw that they walked not rightly to the verity of the Gospel, I said to Cephas before them all: si tu cum Iudaeus sis gentiliter et non iudaice vivis quomodo gentes cogis iudaizareIf thou being a Jew, livest Gentile-like and not Judaically, how doest thou compel the Gentiles to Judaize?
2:15 nos natura Iudaei et non ex gentibus peccatoresWe are by nature Jews, and not of the Gentiles, sinners.
2:16 scientes autem quod non iustificatur homo ex operibus legis nisi per fidem Iesu ChristiBut knowing that man is not justified by the works of the Law, but by the faith of Jesus Christ; et nos in Christo Iesu credidimuswe also believe in Christ Jesus, ut iustificemur ex fide Christi et non ex operibus legisthat we may be justified by the faith of Christ, and not by the works of the Law: propter quod ex operibus legis non iustificabitur omnis carofor the which cause, by the works of the Law no flesh shall be justified.
2:17 absitBut if seeking to be justified in Christ, ourselves also be found sinners; is Christ then a minister of sin? God forbid.
2:18 si enim quae destruxi haec iterum aedifico praevaricatorem me constituoFor if I build the same things again which I have destroyed, I make myself a prevaricator.
2:19 ego enim per legem legi mortuus sumFor I by the Law, am dead to the Law, ut Deo vivamthat I may live to God: Christo confixus sum cruciwith Christ I am nailed to the cross.
2:20 vivo autem iam non egoAnd I live, now not I; vivit vero in me Christusbut Christ liveth in me. And that that I live now in the flesh, I live in the faith of the Son of God, who loved me, and delivered himself for me.
2:21 non abicio gratiam DeiI cast not away the grace of God. si enim per legem iustitia ergo Christus gratis mortuus estFor if justice be by the Law, then Christ died in vain.
3:1 o insensati Galatae quis vos fascinavit ante quorum oculos Iesus Christus proscriptus est crucifixusO senseless Galatians, who hath bewitched you, not to obey the truth, before whose eyes Jesus Christ was proscribed, being crucified among you?
3:2 hoc solum volo a vobis discereThis only I would learn of you: ex operibus legis Spiritum accepistis an ex auditu fideiBy the works of the Law, did you receive the Spirit, or by the hearing of the faith?
3:3 sic stulti estisAre you so foolish, cum Spiritu coeperitis nunc carne consummaminithat whereas you began with the spirit, now you will be consummate with the flesh?
3:4 tanta passi estis sine causa si tamen sine causaHave you suffered so great things without cause? if yet without cause.
3:5 qui ergo tribuit vobis SpiritumHe therefore that giveth you the Spirit, et operatur virtutes in vobisand worketh miracles among you; ex operibus legis an ex auditu fideiby the works of the Law, or by the hearing of the faith doeth he it?
3:6 sicut Abraham credidit DeoAs, et reputatum est ei ad iustitiamAbraham believed God, and it was reputed to him unto justice.
3:7 cognoscitis ergo quia qui ex fide sunt hii sunt filii AbrahaeKnow ye therefore that they that are of faith, the same are the children of Abraham.
3:8 benedicentur in te omnes gentesAnd the Scripture foreseeing that God justifieth the Gentiles by faith, shewed unto Abraham before, That in thee shall all nations be blessed.
3:9 igitur qui ex fide sunt benedicentur cum fideli AbrahamTherefore they that are of faith, shall be blessed with the faithful Abraham.
3:10 quicumque enim ex operibus legis sunt sub maledicto suntFor whosoever are of the works of the Law, scriptum est enimare under curse. maledictus omnis qui non permanserit in omnibus quae scripta sunt in libro legisFor it is written: Cursed be every one that abideth not in all things that be written in the book of the Law, ut faciat eato do them.
3:11 quoniam autem in lege nemo iustificatur apud Deum manifestum est quia iustus ex fide vivitBut that in the Law no man is justified with God, it is manifest, because the just liveth by faith.
3:12 lex autem non est ex fideBut the Law is not by faith: but, He that doeth those things, shall live in them. sed qui fecerit ea vivet in illisChrist hath redeemed us from the curse of the Law, being made a curse for us (because it is written: Cursed is every one that hangeth on a tree) that on the Gentiles the blessing of Abraham might be made in Christ Jesus: that we may receive the promise of the Spirit by faith.
3:15 fratres secundum hominem dicoBrethren (I speak according to man) yet a man';s testament being confirmed no man despiseth, tamen hominis confirmatum testamentum nemo spernitor further disposeth. aut superordinat
3:16 Abrahae dictae sunt promissiones et semini eiusTo Abraham were the promises said, and to his seed. non dicitHe saith not, et seminibusAnd to seeds, quasi in multisas in many: sed quasi in unobut as in one, et semini tuoAnd to thy seed, qui est Christuswhich is Christ.
3:17 hoc autem dicoAnd this I say, testamentum confirmatum a Deo quae post quadringentos et triginta annos facta est lex non irritam facit ad evacuandam promissionemthe testament being confirmed of God, the Law which was made after four hundred and thirty years, maketh not void to frustrate the promise.
3:18 nam si ex lege hereditas iam non ex repromissioneFor if the inheritance be of the Law, now not of promise. Abrahae autem per promissionem donavit DeusBut God gave it to Abraham by promise.
3:19 quid igitur lexWhy was the Law then? It was put for transgressions, propter transgressiones posita estuntil the seed came to whom he had promised: donec veniret semen cui promiseratordained by Angels in the hand of a mediator. ordinata per angelos in manu mediatoris
3:20 mediator autem unius non estAnd a mediator is not of one: Deus autem unus estbut God is one.
3:21 lex ergo adversus promissa DeiWas the Law then against the promises of God? God forbid. absitFor if there had been a Law given that could justify, si enim data esset lex quae posset vivificare vere ex lege esset iustitiaundoubtedly justice should be of the Law.
3:22 sed conclusit scriptura omnia sub peccatoBut the Scripture hath concluded all things under sin: ut promissio ex fide Iesu Christi daretur credentibusthat the promise by the faith of Jesus Christ might be given to them that believe.
3:23 prius autem quam veniret fides sub lege custodiebamur conclusi in eam fidem quae revelanda eratBut before the faith came, under the Law we were kept shut up, unto that faith which was to be revealed.
3:24 itaque lex pedagogus noster fuit in ChristoTherefore the Law was our Pedagogue in Christ: ut ex fide iustificemurthat we may be justified by faith.
3:25 at ubi venit fides iam non sumus sub pedagogoBut when the faith came, now we are not under a pedagogue.
3:26 omnes enim filii Dei estis per fidem in Christo IesuFor you are all the children of God by faith in Christ Jesus.
3:27 quicumque enim in Christo baptizati estis Christum induistisFor as many of you as are baptized in Christ, have put on Christ.
3:28 non est Iudaeus ne que GraecusThere is not Jew nor Greek, non est servus ne que liberthere is not bond nor free, non est masculus ne que feminathere is not male nor female. omnes enim vos unum estis in Christo IesuFor all you are one in Christ Jesus.
3:29 si autem vos Christi ergo Abrahae semen estis secundum promissionem heredesAnd if you be Christ';s, then are you the seed of Abraham, heirs according to promise.
