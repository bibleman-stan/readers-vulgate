1:10 si dixerimus quoniam non peccavimus mendacem facimus eumIf we shall say that we have not sinned, we make him a liar, and his word is not in us. et verbum eius non est in nobis
2:4 qui dicit se nosse eumHe that saith he knoweth him, and keepeth not his commandments, is a liar, and the truth is not in him: et mandata eius non custodit mendax est
2:13 scribo vobis patresI write unto you fathers, because you have known him which is from the beginning. I write unto you young men, because you have overcome the wicked one. quoniam cognovistis eum qui ab initio est scribo vobis adulescentes quoniam vicistis malignum
2:14 scripsi vobis infantesI write to you infants, because you have known the Father. I write unto you young men, because you are strong, and the word of God abideth in you, and you have overcome the wicked one. quoniam cognovistis Patrem scripsi vobis patres quia cognovistis eum qui ab initio scripsi vobis adulescentes quia fortes estis et verbum Dei in vobis manet et vicistis malignum
2:22 quis est mendax nisi is qui negat quoniam Iesus non est ChristusWho is a liar, but he which denieth that Jesus is Christ? This is Antichrist which denieth the Father and the Son.
4:4 vos ex Deo estis filioliYou are of God, little children, and have overcome him. Because greater is he that is in you, than he that is in the world. et vicistis eos quoniam maior est qui in vobis est quam qui in mundo
4:18 sed perfecta caritas foras mittit timoremFear is not in charity: but perfect charity casteth out fear, because fear hath painfulness. And he that feareth, is not perfect in charity. quoniam timor poenam habet qui autem timet non est perfectus in caritate
5:16 non pro illo dico ut rogetHe that knoweth his brother to sin a sin not to death, let him ask, and life shall be given him, sinning not to death. There is a sin to death: for that I say not that any man ask.
