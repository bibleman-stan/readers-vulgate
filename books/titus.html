1:1 Paulus servus Dei apostolus autem Iesu Christi secundum fidem electorum Dei et agnitionem veritatis quae secundum pietatem est in spem vitae aeternae quam promisit qui non mentitur Deus ante tempora saeculariaPaul the servant of God, and an Apostle of Jesus Christ according to the faith of the elect of God and knowledge of the truth: which is according to piety, into the hope of life everlasting, which he promised that lieth not, God, before the secular times:
1:3 manifestavit autem temporibus suis verbum suum in praedicatione quae credita est mihi secundum praeceptum salvatoris nostri Deibut hath manifested in due times his word in preaching, which is committed to me according to the precept of our Saviour God:
1:4 Tito dilecto filio secundum communem fidemto Titus my beloved son according to the common faith, gratia et pax a Deo Patre et Christo Iesu salvatore nostrograce and peace from God the Father, and Christ Jesus our Saviour.
1:5 huius rei gratia reliqui te CretaeFor this cause left I thee in Crete, that thou shouldest reform the things that are wanting, ut ea quae desunt corrigasand shouldest ordain priests by cities, et constituas per civitates presbyterosas I also appointed thee: sicut ego tibi disposuiif any be without crime, si quis sine crimine est unius uxoris vir filios habens fideles non in accusatione luxuriae aut non subditosthe husband of one wife, having faithful children, not in the accusation of riot, or not obedient.
1:7 oportet enim episcopum sine crimine esse sicut Dei dispensatoremFor a Bishop must be without crime, as the steward of God: non superbum non iracundum non vinolentum non percussorem non turpilucri cupidumnot proud, not angry, not given to wine, no striker, not covetous of filthy lucre:
1:8 sed hospitalem benignum sobrium iustum sanctum continentembut given to hospitality, gentle, sober, just, holy, continent:
1:9 amplectentem eum qui secundum doctrinam est fidelem sermonemembracing that faithful word which is according to doctrine, ut potens sit et exhortari in doctrina sana et eos qui contradicunt arguerethat he may be able to exhort in sound doctrine, and to reprove them that gainsay it.
1:10 sunt enim multi et inoboedientes vaniloqui et seductoresFor there be many disobedient, vain-speakers, maxime qui de circumcisione suntand seducers, especially they that are of the Circumcision.
1:11 quos oportet redarguiWho must be controlled. qui universas domos subvertunt docentes quae non oportet turpis lucri gratiaWho subvert whole houses, teaching the things they ought not, for filthy lucre.
1:12 dixit quidam ex illis proprius ipsorum prophetaOne of them said, their own proper prophet, The Cretensians always liars, Cretenses semper mendaces malae bestiae ventres pigrinaughty beasts, slothful bellies.
1:13 testimonium hoc verum estThis testimony is true. quam ob causam increpa illos dure ut sani sint in fide non intendentes iudaicis fabulis et mandatis hominum aversantium se a veritateFor the which cause rebuke them sharply, that they may be sound in the faith, not attending to Jewish fables, and commandments of men, averting themselves from the truth.
1:15 omnia munda mundisAll things are clean to the clean: coinquinatis autem et infidelibus nihil mundumbut to the polluted and to infidels nothing is clean: sed inquinatae sunt eorum et mens et conscientiabut polluted are both their mind and conscience.
1:16 confitentur se nosse DeumThey confess that they know God: factis autem negant cum sunt abominati et incredibiles et ad omne opus bonum reprobibut in their works they deny, whereas they be abominable and incredulous and to every good work reprobate.
3:1 admone illos principibus et potestatibus subditos esse dicto oboedire ad omne opus bonum paratos esseAdmonish them to be subject to Princes and Potestates, to obey at a word, to be ready to every good work,
3:2 neminem blasphemare non litigiosos esse modestos omnem ostendentes mansuetudinem ad omnes hominesto blaspheme no man, not to be litigious, but modest: shewing all mildness toward all men.
3:3 eramus enim et nos aliquando insipientes increduli errantes servientes desideriis et voluptatibus variis in malitia et invidia agentes odibiles odientes invicemFor we also were sometime unwise, incredulous, erring, serving diverse desires and voluptuousnesses, living in malice and envy, odible, hating one another.
3:4 cum autem benignitas et humanitas apparuit salvatoris nostri Dei non ex operibus iustitiae quae fecimus nos sed secundum suam misericordiam salvos nos fecit per lavacrum regenerationis et renovationis Spiritus Sancti quem effudit in nos abunde per Iesum Christum salvatorem nostrum ut iustificati gratia ipsius heredes simus secundum spem vitae aeternaeBut when the benignity and kindness toward man of our Saviour God appeared: not by the works of Justice which we did, but according to his mercy he hath saved us by the laver of regeneration and renovation of the Holy Ghost, whom he hath poured upon us aboundantly by Jesus Christ our Saviour: that being justified by his grace, we may be heirs according to hope of life everlasting.
3:8 fidelis sermo estIt is a faithful saying, et de his volo te confirmareand of these things I will have thee avouch earnestly: ut curent bonis operibus praeesse qui credunt Deothat they which believe in God, be careful to excel in good works. haec sunt bona et utilia hominibusThese things be good and profitable for men.
3:9 stultas autem quaestiones et genealogias et contentiones et pugnas legis devitaBut foolish questions, and genealogies, and contentions, and controversies of the Law avoid. sunt enim inutiles et vanaeFor they are unprofitable and vain.
3:10 hereticum hominem post unam et secundam correptionem devita sciens quia subversus est qui eius modi estAman that is an heretic after the first and second admonition avoid: et delinquit proprio iudicio condemnatusknowing that he that is such an one, is subverted, and sinneth, being condemned by his own judgement.
3:12 cum misero ad te Arteman aut Tychicum festina ad me venire NicopolimWhen I shall send to thee Artemas or Tychicus, hasten to come unto me to Nicopolis. ibi enim statui hiemareFor there I have determined to winter.
3:13 Zenan legis peritum et Apollo sollicite praemitteSet forward Zenas the lawyer and Apollos carefully, ut nihil illis desitthat nothing be wanting to them.
3:14 discant autem et nostri bonis operibus praeesse ad usus necessarios ut non sint infructuosiAnd let our men also learn to excel in good works to necessary uses: that they be not unfruitful.
3:15 salutant te qui me cum sunt omnesAll that are with me, salute thee: saluta qui nos amant in fidesalute them that love us in the faith. gratia Dei cum omnibus vobisThe grace of God be with you all. amenAmen.
