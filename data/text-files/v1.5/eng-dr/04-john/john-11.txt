11:1
And there was a certain sick man, Lazarus of Bethania, of the town of Mary & Martha her sister.

11:2
(And Mary was she that anointed our Lord with ointment, & wiped his feet with her hair: whose brother Lazarus was sick.)



11:3
His sisters therefore sent to him saying: Lord, behold, he whom thou lovest, is sick.


11:4
And Jesus hearing, said to them: This sickness is not to death, but for the glory of God: that the Son of God may be glorified by it.



11:5
And Jesus loved Martha, and her sister Mary, and Lazarus.

11:6
As he heard therefore that he was sick, then he tarried in the same place two days:

11:7
then after this he saith to his Disciples: Let us go into Jewry again.


11:8
The Disciples say to him: Rabbi, now the Jews sought to stone thee; and goest thou thither again?



11:9
Jesus answered: Are there not twelve hours of the day? If a man walk in the day, he stumbleth not: because he seeth the light of this world:



11:10
but if he walk in the night, he stumbleth, because the light is not in him.

11:11
These things he said; and after this he saith to them: Lazarus our friend sleepeth; but I go that I may raise him from sleep.





11:12
His Disciples therefore said: Lord, if he sleep, he shall be safe.


11:13
But Jesus spake of his death; & they thought that he spake of the sleeping of sleep.


11:14
Then therefore Jesus said to them plainly: Lazarus is dead;


11:15
and I am glad for your sake, that you may believe, because I was not there: but let us go to him.


11:16
Thomas therefore, who is called Didymus, said to his condisciples: Let us also go, to die with him.



11:17
Jesus therefore came, and found him now having been four days in the grave.


11:18
(And Bethania was nigh to Jerusalem about fifteen furlongs.)

11:19
And many of the Jews were come to Martha and Mary, to comfort them concerning their brother.


11:20
Martha therefore when she heard that Jesus was come, went to meet him; but Mary sat at home.


11:21
Martha therefore said to Jesus: Lord if thou hadst been here, my brother had not died.


11:22
But now also I know that what things soever thou shalt ask of God, God will give thee.

11:23
Jesus saith to her: Thy brother shall rise again.


11:24
Martha saith to him: I know that he shall rise again in the resurrection, in the last day.


11:25
Jesus saith to her: I am the resurrection and the life; he that believeth in me, although he be dead, shall live.



11:26
And every one that liveth, and believeth in me, shall not die for ever. Believest thou this?


11:27
She saith to him: Yea Lord, I have believed that thou art Christ the Son of God that art come into this world.


11:28
And when she had said these things, she went, and called Mary her sister secretly, saying: The Master is come, & calleth thee.




11:29
She, when she heard, riseth quickly & cometh to him.


11:30
For Jesus was not yet come into the town: but he was yet in that place where Martha had met him.


11:31
The Jews therefore that were with her in the house and did comfort her, when they saw Mary that she rose quickly and went forth, followed her, saying: That she goeth to the grave, to weep there.





11:32
Mary therefore when she was come where Jesus was, seeing him, fell at his feet, and saith to him: Lord, if thou hadst been here, my brother had not died.





11:33
Jesus therefore when he saw her, weeping, and the Jews that were come with her, weeping, he groaned in spirit, and troubled himself,



11:34
and said: Where have you laid him? They say to him: Lord, come and see.




11:35
And Jesus wept.

11:36
The Jews therefore said: Behold how he loved him.


11:37
But certain of them said: Could not he that opened the eyes of the blind man, make that this man should not die?



11:38
Jesus therefore again groaning in himself, cometh to the grave: and it was a cave; and a stone was laid over it.



11:39
Jesus saith: Take away the stone. Martha the sister of him that was dead, said to him: Lord, now he stinketh, for he is now of four days.





11:40
Jesus saith to her: Did not I say to thee, that if thou believe, thou shalt see the glory of God?


11:41
They took therefore the stone away. And Jesus lifting his eyes upward, said: Father, I give thee thanks that thou hast heard me.




11:42
And I did know that thou doest always hear me, but for the people that standeth about, have I said it, that they may believe that thou hast sent me.



11:43
When he had said these things, he cried with a loud voice: Lazarus, come forth.


11:44
And forthwith he came forth that had been dead, bound feet and hands with winding bands, and his face was tied with a napkin. Jesus said to them: Loose him, and let him go.





11:45
Many therefore of the Jews that were come to Mary & Martha, & had seen the things that Jesus did, believed in him.

11:46
And certain of them went to the Pharisees, & told them the things that Jesus did.


11:47
The chief Priests therefore & the Pharisees gathered a Council, & said: What do we, for this man doeth many signs.



11:48
If we let him alone so, all will believe in him: & the Romans will come, & take away our place & nation.



11:49
But one of them named Caiphas, being the high Priest of that year, said to them: You know nothing,




11:51
And this he said not of himself: but being the high Priest of that year, he prophesied that Jesus should die for the nation.


11:53
From that day therefore they devised to kill him.

11:54
Jesus therefore walked no more openly among the Jews, but he went into the country beside the desert unto a city that is called Ephrem, and there he abode with his Disciples.



11:55
And the Pasch of the Jews was at hand: & many of the country went up to Jerusalem before the Pasch to sanctify themselves.



11:56
They sought Jesus therefore; and they communed one with another, standing in the temple: What think you, in that he is not come to the festival day? And the chief Priests & Pharisees had given commandment, that if any man should know where he was, he should tell, that they might apprehend him.
