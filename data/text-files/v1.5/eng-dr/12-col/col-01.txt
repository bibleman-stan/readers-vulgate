1:1
Paul an Apostle of Jesus Christ by the will of God, and brother Timothee:

1:2
to them that are at Colossa saints and faithful brethren in Christ Jesus.

1:3
Grace to you and peace from God our Father and our Lord Jesus Christ. We give thanks to God and the Father of our Lord Jesus Christ always for you, praying:


1:13
Who hath delivered us from the power of darkness, and hath translated us into the Kingdom of the Son of his love,


1:15
who is the image of the invisible God, the first-born of all creature:

1:16
because in him were created all things in Heaven, and in earth, visible, and invisible, whether Thrones, or Dominations, or Principalities, or Potestates: all by him, & in him were created:

1:17
and he is before all, and all consist in him.


1:18
And he is the Head of the body, the Church, who is the beginning, first-born of the dead: that he may be in all things holding the primacy:

1:19
because in him it hath well pleased, all fullness to inhabit:


1:21
And you, whereas you were sometime alienated and enemies in sense, in evil works:


1:24
Who now rejoice in suffering for you, and do accomplish those things that want of the passions of Christ, in my flesh for his body, which is the Church:


1:25
whereof I am made a minister according to the dispensation of God, which is given me toward you, that I may fulfill the word of God,


1:26
the mystery that hath been hidden from worlds and generations, but now is manifested to his saints,

1:29
Wherein also I labour striving according to his operation which he worketh in me in power.
