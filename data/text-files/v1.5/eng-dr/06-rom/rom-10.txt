10:1
Brethren, the will of my heart surely and prayer to God, is for them unto salvation.

10:2
For I give them testimony that they have zeal of God, but not according to knowledge.

10:3
For not knowing the justice of God, and seeking to establish their own, they have not been subject to the justice of God.

10:4
For, the end of the Law is Christ; unto justice to every one that believeth.

10:5
For Moyses wrote, that, the justice which is of the Law, the man that hath done it, shall live in it.

10:6
But the justice which is of faith,
saith thus:
Say not in thy heart,
Who shall ascend into Heaven? that is to bring Christ down.

10:7
Or who descendeth into the depth? that is to call Christ again from the dead.


10:8
But what saith the Scripture? The word is nigh,
in thy mouth, and in thy heart.
This is the word of faith which we preach.

10:9
For if thou confess with thy mouth our Lord Jesus, and in thy heart believe that God hath raised him up from the dead, thou shalt be saved.

10:10
For with the heart we believe unto justice;
but with the mouth confession is made to salvation.

10:11
For the Scripture saith:
Whosoever believeth in him, shall not be confounded.

10:12
For there is no distinction of the Jew and the Greek:
for one is Lord of all, rich toward all that invocate him.

10:13
For every one whosoever shall invocate the name of our Lord, shall be saved.

10:14
How then shall they invocate in whom they have not believed? Or how shall they believe him whom they have not heard? And how shall they hear without a preacher?



10:15
But how shall they preach unless they be sent? as it is written:
How beautiful are the feet of them that evangelize peace,
of them that evangelize good things?



10:16
But all do not obey the Gospel.
For Esay saith, Lord, who hath believed the hearing of us?

10:17
Faith then, is by hearing:
and hearing is by the word of Christ.

10:18
But I say,
have they not heard? And certes into all the earth hath the sound of them gone forth:
and unto the ends of the whole world the words of them.

10:19
But I say,
hath not Israel known? Moyses first saith:
I will bring you to emulation in that which is not a nation:
in a foolish nation,
I will drive you into anger.

10:20
But Esay is bold,
and saith:
I was found of them that did not seek me:
openly I appeared to them that asked not of me.

10:21
But to Israel he saith:
All the day have I spread my hands to a people that believeth not, and contradicteth me.
