1:1
Paul an Apostle not of men, neither by man, but by Jesus Christ, and God the Father that raised him from the dead,



1:3
Grace to you and peace from God the Father and our Lord Jesus Christ,


1:5
to whom is glory for ever and ever. Amen.

1:6
I marvel that thus so soon you are transferred from him that called you into the grace of Christ, unto another Gospel:

1:8
But although we, or an Angel from Heaven, evangelize to you beside that which we have evangelized to you, be he anathema.



1:9
As we have said before, so now I say again: If any evangelize to you, beside that which you have received, be he anathema.



1:10
For do I now use persuasion to men, or to God? Or do I seek to please men? If I yet did please men, I should not be the servant of Christ.



1:11
For I do you to understand, brethren, the Gospel that was evangelized of me, that it is not according to man.

1:12
For neither did I receive it of man, nor learn it; but by the revelation of Jesus Christ.



1:15
But when it pleased him that separated me from my mother's womb, and called me by his grace, to reveal his Son in me,







1:18
Then, after three years I came to Jerusalem to see Peter: and tarried with him fifteen days.


1:19
But other of the Apostles saw I none; saving James the brother of our Lord.

1:20
And the things that I write to you; behold before God, that I lie not.

1:21
After that, I came into the parts of Syria and Cilicia.

1:22
And I was unknown by sight to the churches of Jewry, that were in Christ:

1:23
but they had heard only, That he which persecuted us sometime, doth now evangelize the faith which sometime he expugned:


1:24
and in me they glorified God.
