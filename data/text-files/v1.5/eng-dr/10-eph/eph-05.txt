5:1
Be ye therefore followers of God, as most dear children:




5:5
For understanding know you this, that no fornicator, or unclean, or covetous person (which is the service of idols) hath inheritance in the Kingdom of Christ and of God.

5:6
Let no man seduce you with vain words. For, for these things cometh the anger of God upon the children of diffidence.


5:7
Become not therefore partakers with them.

5:8
For you were sometime darkness, but now light in our Lord. Walk as children of the light,


5:11
and communicate not with the unfruitful works of darkness, but rather reprove them.


5:12
For the things that are done of them in secret, it is shame even to speak.

5:13
But all things that are reproved, are manifested by the light. For all that is manifested, is light.


5:14
For the which cause he saith: Rise thou that sleepest, and arise from the dead: and Christ will illuminate thee.




5:17
Therefore become not unwise, but understanding what is the will of God.

5:18
And be not drunk with wine wherein is riotousness, but be filled with the Spirit,


5:22
Let women be subject to their husbands, as to our Lord:

5:23
because the man is the head of the woman: as Christ is the head of the Church. Himself, the Saviour of his body.

5:24
But as the Church is subject to Christ, so also the women to their husbands in all things.

5:25
Husbands, love your wives, as Christ also loved the Church, and delivered himself for it:





5:28
So also men ought to love their wives as their own bodies. He that loveth his wife, loveth himself.


5:29
For no man ever hated his own flesh: but he nourisheth and cherisheth it, as also Christ the Church:



5:30
because we be the members of his body, of his flesh, and of his bones.

5:31
For this cause shall man leave his father and mother: and shall cleave to his wife, and they shall be two in one flesh.



5:32
This is a great Sacrament. But I speak in Christ & in the Church.


5:33
Nevertheless you also every one, let each love his wife as himself: and let the wife fear her husband.
