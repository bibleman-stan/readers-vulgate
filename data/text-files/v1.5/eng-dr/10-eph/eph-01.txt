1:1
Paul an Apostle of Jesus Christ by the will of God: to all the saints that are at Ephesus; & to the faithful in Christ Jesus.

1:2
Grace to you and peace from God our Father, and our Lord Jesus Christ.

1:3
Blessed be God and the Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, which hath blessed us in all spiritual blessing, in celestials, in Christ:


1:5
Who hath predestinated us unto the adoption of sons, by Jesus Christ, unto himself; according to the purpose of his will:

1:7
In whom we have redemption by his blood (the remission of sins) according to the riches of his grace.


1:11
In whom we also are called by lot: predestinate according to the purpose of him that worketh all things, according to the counsel of his will:

1:13
in whom you also, when you had heard the word of truth (the Gospel of your salvation) in which also believing you were signed with the holy Spirit of promise,

1:15
Therefore I also hearing your faith that is in our Lord Jesus, and love toward all the saints,


1:18
the eyes of your heart illuminated, that you may know what the hope is of his vocation, and what are the riches of the glory of his inheritance in the saints,






1:22
And he hath subdued all things under his feet: and hath made him Head over all the Church,
