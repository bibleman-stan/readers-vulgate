6:1
Children, obey your parents in our Lord. For this is just.


6:2
Honour thy father & thy mother (which is the first commandment in the promise)




6:4
And you fathers, provoke not your children to anger: but bring them up in the discipline & correption of our Lord.


6:5
Servants, be obedient to your lords according to the flesh, with fear and trembling, in the simplicity of your heart, as to Christ:


6:9
And you masters, do the same things to them, remitting threatenings: knowing that both their Lord and yours, is in Heaven: and acception of persons is not with him.


6:10
Hence-forth, brethren, be strengthened in our Lord, and in the might of his power.

6:11
Put you on the armour of God, that you may stand against the deceits of the devil.


6:12
For our wrestling is not against flesh and blood: but against Princes and Potestates, against the rectors of the world of this darkness, against the spirituals of wickedness in the celestials.

6:13
Therefore take the armour of God, that you may resist in the evil day, and stand in all things perfect.


6:14
Stand therefore having your loins girded in truth, and clothed with the breast-plate of justice,


6:17
And take unto you the helmet of salvation: and the sword of the spirit (which is the word of God)





6:20
for the which I am a legate in this chain, so that in it I may be bold according as I ought, to speak.



6:21
And that you also may know the things about me, what I do, Tychicus my dearest brother and faithful minister in our Lord, will make you understand all things:

6:22
whom I have sent to you for this same purpose, that you may know the things about us, and he may comfort your hearts.


6:23
Peace to the brethren and charity with faith from God the Father, and our Lord Jesus Christ.

6:24
Grace with all that love our Lord Jesus Christ in incorruption. Amen.
