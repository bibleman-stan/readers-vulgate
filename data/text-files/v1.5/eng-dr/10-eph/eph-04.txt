4:1
I therefore prisoner in our Lord, beseech you, that you walk worthy of the vocation in which you are called,

4:4
One body & one spirit: as you are called in one hope of your vocation.

4:5
One Lord, one faith, one baptism.

4:6
One God and Father of all, which is over all, and by all, & in all us.

4:7
But to every one of us is given grace according to the measure of the donation of Christ.

4:8
For the which he saith: Ascending on high he led captivity captive: he gave gifts to men.



4:9
(And that he ascended, what is it, but because he descended also first into the inferior parts of the earth?


4:10
He that descended, the same is also he that is ascended above all the Heavens, that he might fill all things.)


4:16
of whom the whole body being compact and knit together by all juncture of subministration, according to the operation in the measure of every member, maketh the increase of the body unto the edifying of it-self in charity.

4:17
This therefore I say and testify in our Lord: that now you walk not as also the Gentiles walk in the vanity of their sense,



4:20
But you have not so learned Christ:



4:23
And be renewed in the spirit of your mind:


4:25
For the which cause laying away lying, speak ye truth every one with is neighbour, because we are members one of another.

4:26
Be angry and sin not. Let not the sun go down upon your anger.



4:27
Give not place to the devil.

4:28
He that stole, let him now not steal: but rather let him labour in working with his hands that which is good, that he may have whence to give unto him that suffereth necessity.



4:30
And contristate not the holy Spirit of God: in which you are signed unto the day of redemption.


4:31
Let all bitterness, and anger, and indignation, and clamour, and blasphemy be taken away from you with all malice.

4:32
And be gentle one to another, merciful, pardoning one another, as also God in Christ hath pardoned you.
