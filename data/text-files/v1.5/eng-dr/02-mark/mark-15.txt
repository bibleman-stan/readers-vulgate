15:1
And forthwith in the morning the chief Priests with the Ancients and the Scribes & the whole Council, consulting together,
binding Jesus led & delivered him to Pilate.

15:2
And Pilate asked him:
Art thou the King of the Jews? but he answering,
said to him:
Thou sayest,

15:3
And the chief Priests accused him in many things.

15:4
And Pilate again asked him,
saying:
Answerest thou nothing? See in how many things they accuse thee.

15:5
But Jesus answered nothing more;
so that Pilate marveled.

15:6
And upon the festival day he was wont to release unto them one of the prisoners whomsoever they had demanded.

15:7
And there was one called Barabbas, which was put in prison with seditious persons, who in a sedition had committed murder.

15:8
And when the multitude was come up, they began to require, according as always he did unto them.

15:9
And Pilate answered them,
and said:
Will you that I release to you the King of the Jews?

15:10
For he knew that the chief Priests for envy had delivered him.

15:11
But the chief Priests moved the people,
that he should release Barabbas rather to them.

15:12
And Pilate again answering, said to them:
What will you then that I do to the King of the Jews?

15:13
But they again cried:
Crucify him.

15:14
And Pilate saith to them:
Why,
what evil hath he done? But they cried the more:
Crucify him.

15:15
And Pilate willing to satisfy the people, released to them Barabbas,
and delivered Jesus, having whipped him,
for to be crucified.

15:16
And the soldiers led him into the court of the Palace, and they call together the whole band:
and they clothe him in purple,
and platting a crown of thorns,
they put it upon him.

15:18
And they began to salute him:
Hail King of the Jews.

15:19
And they smote his head with a reed:
and they did spit on him.
And bowing the knees, they adored him.

15:20
And after they had mocked him,
they stripped him of the purple,
and put on him his own garments,
and they lead him forth to crucify him.

15:21
And they forced a certain man that passed by, Simon a Cyrenean coming from the country, the father of Alexander and Rufus,
to take up his cross.

15:22
And they bring him into the place Golgotha, which being interpreted is, The place of Calvary.

15:23
And they gave him to drink wine mingled with myrrh;
and he took it not.

15:24
And crucifying him, they divided his garments, casting lots upon them, who should take which.

15:25
And it was the third hour,
and they crucified him.

15:26
And the title of his cause was superscribed,
King of The Jews.

15:27
And with him they crucify two thieves;
one on the right hand, and another on his left.

15:28
And the Scripture was fulfilled that saith:
And with the wicked he was reputed.

15:29
And they that passed by, blasphemed him,
wagging their heads,
and saying:
Vah, he that destroyeth the Temple,
and in three days buildeth it: save thyself, coming down from the cross.

15:31
In like manner also the chief Priests mocking said with the Scribes one to another:
He saved others,
himself he cannot save.

15:32
Let Christ the King of Israel come down now from the cross that we may see and believe.
And they that were crucified with him,
railed at him.


15:33
And when it was the sixth hour, there was made darkness upon the whole earth until the ninth hour.

15:34
And at the ninth hour Jesus cried out with a mighty voice, saying: Eloi,
Eloi,
lamma-sabacthani? Which is being interpreted,
My God, my God, why hast thou forsaken me?

15:35
And certain of the standers about hearing, said:
Behold, he calleth Elias.

15:36
And one running and filling a spunge with vinegar, and putting it about a reed, gave him drink,
saying:
Let be, let us see if Elias come to take him down.

15:37
And Jesus putting forth a mighty voice, gave up the ghost.

15:38
And the veil of the temple was rent in two, from the top to the bottom.

15:39
And the Centurion that stood over against him, seeing that so crying he had given up the ghost, said:
Indeed this man was the Son of God.

15:40
And there were also women looking on a far off:
among whom was Mary Magdalene, and Mary the mother of James the less, and of Joseph, and Salome;

15:41
and when he was in Galilee,
they followed him, and ministered to him, and many other women that came up together with him to Jerusalem.

15:42
And when evening was now come (because it was the Parasceve, which is the Sabboth-eve) came Joseph of Arimathæa a noble Senator, who himself also was expecting the Kingdom of God;
and he went in boldly to Pilate,
and asked the body of Jesus.

15:44
But Pilate marveled if he were now dead.
And sending for the Centurion, asked him if he were now dead.

15:45
And when he understood by the Centurion, he gave the body to Joseph.

15:46
And Joseph buying sindon, and taking him down,
wrapped him in the sindon, and laid him in a monument,
that was hewed out of a rock. And he rolled a stone to the door of the monument.

15:47
And Mary Magdalene and Mary of Joseph beheld where he was laid.
