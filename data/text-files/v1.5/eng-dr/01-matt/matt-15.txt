15:1
Then came to him from Jerusalem Scribes and Pharisees, saying:

15:2
Why do thy Disciples transgress the tradition of the Ancients? For they wash not their hands when they eat bread.



15:3
But he answering said to them:
Why do you also transgress the commandment of God for your tradition? For God said:

15:4
Honour father and mother.
And:
He that shall curse father or mother, dying let him die.

15:5
But you say:
whosoever shall say to father or mother,
the gift whatsoever proceedeth from me, shall profit thee:

15:6
And shall not honour his father or his mother:
& you have made frustrate the commandment of God for your own tradition.

15:7
Hypocrites, well hath Esay Prophesied of you, saying:

15:8
This People honoureth me with their lips:
but their heart is far from me.

15:9
And in vain do they worship me, teaching doctrines and commandments of men.

15:10
And having called together the multitudes unto him,
he said to them:
Hear ye and understand.

15:11
Not that which entereth into the mouth, defileth a man:
but that which proceedeth out of the mouth, that defileth a man.

15:12
Then came his Disciples, and said to him:
Dost thou know that the Pharisees, when they heard this word, were scandalized?

15:13
But he answering said:
All planting which my Heavenly Father hath not planted, shall be rooted up.

15:14
Let them alone:
blind they are, guides of the blind.
And if the blind be guide to the blind, both fall into the ditch.

15:15
And Peter answering said to him:
Expound us this parable.

15:16
But he said:
Are you also as yet without understanding?

15:17
Do you not understand, that all that entereth into the mouth, goeth into the belly, and is cast forth into the privy?

15:18
But the things that proceed out of the mouth, come forth from the heart,
and those things defile a man.

15:19
For from the heart come forth evil cogitations, murders, adulteries, fornications, thefts, false testimonies, blasphemies.

15:20
These are the things that defile a man.
But to eat with unwashen hands, doth not defile a man.

15:21
And Jesus went forth from thence and retired into the quarters of Tyre and Sidon.

15:22
And behold a woman of Chanaan came forth out of those coasts, & crying out, said to him:
Have mercy upon me, O Lord the Son of David:
my daughter is sore vexed of a devil.

15:23
Who answered her not a word.
And his Disciples came and besought him saying:
Dismiss her, because she cryeth out after us.

15:24
And he answering said:
I was not sent but to the sheep that are lost of the house of Israel.

15:25
But she came and adored him,
saying: Lord,
help me.

15:26
Who answering, said:
It is not good to take the bread of the Children, and to cast it to the dogs.

15:27
But she said:
Yea Lord;
for the whelps also eat of the crumbs that fall from the table of their masters.

15:28
Then Jesus answering said to her:
O woman,
great is thy faith:
be it done to thee as thou wilt:
And her daughter was made whole from that hour.

15:29
And when Jesus was passed from thence, he came beside the sea of Galilee:
& ascending into the mountain, sat there.

15:30
And there came to him great multitudes, having with them dumb persons, blind, lame,
feeble,
and many others:
and they cast them down at his feet, and he cured them:

15:31
So that the multitudes marveled seeing the dumb speak, the lame walk, the blind see: and they magnified the God of Israel.

15:32
And Jesus called together his Disciples,
and said:
I pity the multitude because three days now they continue with me,
& have not what to eat:
and dismiss them fasting I will not,
lest they faint in the way.

15:33
And the Disciples say unto him:
whence then may we get so many loaves in the desert as to fill so great a multitude?

15:34
And Jesus said to them:
How many loaves have you? But they said:
Seven,
& a few little fishes.

15:35
And he commanded the multitude to sit down upon the ground.

15:36
And taking the Seven loaves & the fishes, and giving thanks, he brake,
& gave it to his Disciples,
and the Disciples gave to the people.

15:37
And they did all eat,
and had their fill.
And that which was left of the fragments they took up, seven baskets full.

15:38
And there were that did eat, four thousand men, beside children & women.

15:39
And having dismissed the multitude, he went up into a boat,
and came into the coasts of Magedan.
