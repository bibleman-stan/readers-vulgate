3:1
And in those days cometh John the Baptist preaching in the desert of Jewry,

3:2
& saying: Do penance:
for the Kingdom of Heaven is at hand.

3:3
For this is he that was spoken of by Esay the Prophet, saying:
A voice of one crying in the desert,
prepare ye the way of our Lord,
make straight his paths.

3:4
And the said John had his garment of camels' hair, and a girdle of a skin about his loins:
and his meat was locusts & wild honey.

3:5
Then went forth to him Jerusalem and all Jewry,
and all the country about Jordan:
& were baptized of him in Jordan, confessing their sins.

3:7
And seeing many of the Pharisees and Sadducees coming to his Baptism, he said to them.
Ye viper's brood, who hath shewed you to flee from the wrath to come?

3:8
Yield therefore fruit worthy of penance.

3:9
And delight not to say within yourselves,
we have Abraham to our father.
For I tell you that God is able of these stones to raise up children to Abraham.

3:10
For now the axe is put to the root of the trees.
Every tree therefore that doth not yield good fruit, shall be cut down,
& cast into the fire.

3:11
I indeed baptize you in water unto penance,
but he that shall come after me, is stronger than I, whose shoes I am not worthy to bear,
he shall baptize you in the Holy Ghost & fire.

3:12
Whose fan is in his hand,
and he shall clean purge his floor:
and he will gather his wheat into the barn,
but the chaff he will burn with unquenchable fire.

3:13
Then cometh Jesus from Galilee to Jordan, unto John,
to be baptized of him.

3:14
But John stayed him, saying:
I ought to be baptized of thee,
and comest thou to me?

3:15
And Jesus answering, said to him:
Suffer me for this time.
For so it becometh us to fulfill all justice.
Then he suffered him.

3:16
And Jesus being baptized, forthwith came out of the water:
and lo
the Heavens were opened to him:
& he saw the Spirit of God descending as a dove, & coming upon him.

3:17
And behold a voice from Heaven saying:
This is my beloved Son, in whom I am well pleased.
