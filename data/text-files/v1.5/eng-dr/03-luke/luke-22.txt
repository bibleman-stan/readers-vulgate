22:1
And the festival day of the Azymes approached, which is called Pasch:

22:2
& the chief Priests & the Scribes sought how they might kill him: but they feared the people.


22:3
And Satan entered into Judas that was surnamed Iscariote, one of the Twelve.

22:4
And he went, and talked with the chief Priests and the Magistrates, how he might betray him to them.


22:5
And they were glad, and bargained to give him money.


22:6
And he promised. And he sought opportunity to betray him apart from the multitudes.


22:7
And the day of the Azymes came, wherein it was necessary that the Pasch should be killed.

22:8
And he sent Peter and John, saying: Go and prepare us the Pasch, that we may eat.



22:9
But they said: Where wilt thou that we prepare it?


22:10
And he said to them: Behold, as you enter into the city, there shall meet you a man carrying a pitcher of water: follow him into the house into which he entereth,




22:11
and you shall say to the good-man of the house: The Master saith to thee, where is the inn where I may eat the Pasch with my Disciples?


22:12
And he will shew you a great refectory adorned: and there prepare.


22:13
And they going, found as he said to them, and prepared the Pasch.


22:14
And when the hour was come, he sat down, and the twelve Apostles with him.

22:15
And he said to them: With desire I have desired to eat this Pasch with you before I suffer.



22:16
For I say to you, that from this time I will not eat it, till it be fulfilled in the Kingdom of God.


22:17
And taking the chalice he gave thanks, and said: Take and divide among you.




22:18
For I say to you, that I will not drink of the generation of the vine, till the Kingdom of God do come.


22:19
And taking bread, he gave thanks, and brake; and gave to them, saying: This is my Body Which is Given For You. Do this for a commemoration of me.





22:20
In like manner the chalice also, after he had supped, saying: This is The Chalice The New Testament in my Blood, Which Shall be Shed For You.


22:21
But yet behold, the hand of him that betrayeth me, is with me on the table.

22:22
And the Son of man indeed goeth according to that which is determined: but yet woe to that man by whom he shall be betrayed.


22:23
And they began to question among themselves, which of them it should be that should do this.

22:24
And there fell also a contention between them, which of them seemed to be greater.

22:25
And he said to them: The Kings of the Gentiles overrule them; and they that have power upon them, are called beneficial.



22:26
But you not so: but he that is the greater among you, let him become as the younger: & he that is the leader, as the waiter.



22:27
For which is greater, he that sitteth at the table, or he that ministereth? Is not he that sitteth? But I am in the midst of you, as he that ministereth:





22:28
& you are they that have remained with me in my tentations.

22:29
And I dispose to you, as my Father disposed to me, a Kingdom:





22:31
And our Lord said: Simon, Simon, behold Satan hath required to have you for to sift as wheat:


22:32
But I have Prayed For Thee, that thy faith fail not: and thou once converted, confirm thy brethren.


22:33
Who said to him: Lord, with thee I am ready to go both into prison and unto death.


22:34
And he said: I say to thee Peter, the cock shall not crow today, till thou deny thrice that thou knowest me.




22:35
And he said to them: When I sent you without purse and scrip and shoes, did you lack any thing? But they said: Nothing.




22:36
He said therefore unto them: But now he that hath a purse, let him take it, likewise also a scrip: and he that hath not, let him sell his coat, and buy a sword.




22:37
For I say to you, that yet this that is written must be fulfilled in me: And with the wicked was he reputed. For those things that are concerning me, have an end.



22:38
But they said: Lord, lo two swords here. But he said to them: It is enough.




22:39
And going forth he went according to his custom into mount-Olivet. And his Disciples also followed him.


22:40
And when he was come to the place, he said to them: Pray, lest ye enter into tentation.


22:41
And he was pulled away from them a stone's cast: and kneeling he prayed,


22:42
saying: Father, if thou wilt, transfer this chalice from me. But yet not my will, but thine be done.


22:43
And there appeared to him an Angel from Heaven, strengthening him. And being in an agony, he prayed the longer.


22:44
And his sweat became as drops of blood trickling down upon the earth.

22:45
And when he was risen up from prayer, and was come to his Disciples, he found them sleeping for pensiveness.



22:46
And he said to them: Why sleep you? Arise, pray, lest you enter into tentation.




22:47
As he was yet speaking, behold a multitude: and he that was called Judas, one of the Twelve, went before them, and approached to Jesus, for to kiss him.




22:48
And Jesus said to him: Judas with a kiss doest thou betray the Son of man?


22:49
And they that were about him, seeing what would be, said to him: Lord, shall we strike with the sword?


22:50
And one of them smote the servant of the high Priest: and cut off his right ear.


22:51
But Jesus answering, said: Suffer ye thus far. And when he had touched his ear, he healed him.



22:52
And Jesus said to them that were come unto him, the chief Priests, and magistrates of the temple, & ancients: As it were to a thief are you come forth with swords and clubs?


22:53
When I was daily with you in the temple, you did not lay hands upon me, but this is your hour, and the power of darkness.


22:54
And apprehending him, they led him to the high Priest's house: but Peter followed a far off.


22:55
And a fire being kindled in the midst of the court, & they sitting about it, Peter was in the midst of them.

22:56
Whom when a certain wench saw sitting at the light, and had beheld him, she said: This fellow also was with him.




22:57
But he denied him, saying: Woman, I know him not.


22:58
And after a while another man seeing him, said: And thou art of them. But Peter said: O man I am not.




22:59
And after the space as it were of one hour, a certain other man affirmed, saying: Verily this fellow also was with him: for he is also a Galilæan.



22:60
And Peter said: Man I know not what thou sayest. And incontinent as he was yet speaking, the cock crew.



22:61
And our Lord turning looked on Peter. And Peter remembered the word of our Lord, as he had said: That before the cock crow, thou shalt thrice deny me.



22:62
And Peter going forth a doors, wept bitterly.

22:63
And the men that held him, mocked him, beating him.

22:64
And they did blind-fold him, and smote his face. And they asked him saying: Prophesy, who is it that smote thee?




22:65
And blaspheming many other things they said against him.

22:66
And when it was day, there assembled the Ancients of the people and chief Priests and Scribes, and they brought him into their Council, saying:



22:67
If thou be Christ tell us. And he said to them: If I tell you, you will not believe me:


22:68
if also I ask, you will not answer me, nor dismiss me.


22:69
But from henceforth the Son of man shall be sitting on the right hand of the power of God.

22:70
And they all said: Art thou then the Son of God? Who said: You say that I am.




22:71
But they said: What need we testimony any further? For ourselves have heard of his own mouth.
