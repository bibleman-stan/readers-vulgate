18:1
And he spake also a parable to them that it behoveth always to pray, & not to be weary,

18:2
saying: There was a certain judge in a certain city, which feared not God, and of man made no account.


18:3
And there was a certain widow in that city, and she came to him, saying: Revenge me of mine adversary.



18:4
And he would not of a long time. But afterward he said within himself: Although I fear not God, nor make account of man,






18:6
And our Lord said: Hear what the judge of iniquity sayeth.


18:7
And will not God revenge his elect that cry to him day and night: and will he have patience in them?


18:8
Isay to you that he will quickly revenge them. But yet the Son of man coming, shall he find trow you, faith in the earth?




18:9
And he said also to certain that trusted in themselves as just, and despised others, this parable:



18:10
Two men went up into the Temple to pray: the one a Pharisee, and the other a Publican.



18:11
The Pharisee standing, prayed thus with himself: God, I give thee thanks that I am not as the rest of men, extortioners, unjust, adulterers, as also this Publican.



18:12
I fast twice in a week: I give tithes of all that I possess.


18:13
And the Publican standing a far off would not so much as lift up his eyes toward Heaven; but he knocked his breast, saying: God be merciful to me a sinner.



18:14
Isay to you, this man went down into his house justified more than he: because every one that exalteth himself, shall be humbled; and he that humbleth himself, shall be exalted.




18:15
And they brought unto him infants also, that he might touch them. Which thing when the Disciples saw, they rebuked them.



18:16
But Jesus calling them together, said: Suffer children to come unto me, and forbid them not, for the Kingdom of Heaven is for such.




18:17
Amen I say to you: Whosoever receiveth not the Kingdom of God as a child, shall not enter into it.


18:18
And a certain Prince asked him, saying: Good Master, by doing what, shall I possess everlasting life?


18:19
And Jesus said to him: Why doest thou call me good? None is good but only God.



18:20
Thou knowest the commandments: Thou shalt not kill, Thou shalt not commit adultery, Thou shalt not steal, Thou shalt not bear false witness, Honour thy father & mother.






18:21
Who said: All these things have I kept from my youth.


18:22
Which Jesus hearing, said to him: Yet one thing thou lackest: Sell all that ever thou hast, & give to the poor, and thou shalt have treasure in Heaven: and come follow me.







18:23
He hearing these things, was stroken sad: because he was very rich.

18:24
And Jesus seeing him stroken sad, said: How hardly shall they that have money enter into the Kingdom of God?


18:25
For it is easier for a camel to pass through the eye of a needle, than for a rich man to enter into the Kingdom of God.

18:26
And they that heard, said: And who can be saved?


18:27
He said to them: The things that are impossible with men, are possible with God.


18:28
And Peter said: Lo, we have left all things, and have followed thee.



18:29
Who said to them: Amen I say to you, there is no man that hath left house, or parents, or brethren, or wife, or children for the Kingdom of God,




18:31
And Jesus took the Twelve, and said to them: Behold we go up to Jerusalem, and all things shall be consummate which were written by the Prophets of the Son of man.




18:32
For he shall be delivered to the Gentiles, and shall be mocked, and scourged, and spit upon:




18:33
and after they have scourged him, they will kill him, and the third day he shall rise again.


18:34
And they understood none of these things, and this word was hid from them, and they understood not the things that were said.



18:35
And it came to pass, when he drew nigh to Jericho, a certain blind man sat by the way, begging.



18:36
And when he heard the multitude passing by, he asked what this should be.

18:37
And they told him that Jesus of Nazareth passed by.

18:38
And he cried saying: Jesus son of David, have mercy upon me.


18:39
And they that went before, rebuked him, that he should hold his peace. But he cried much more, Son of David have mercy upon me.




18:40
And Jesus standing commanded him to be brought unto him. And when he was come near, he asked him,


18:41
saying: What wilt thou that I do to thee? but he said: Lord, that I may see.



18:42
And Jesus said to him: Do thou see; thy faith hath made thee whole.



18:43
And forthwith he saw, and followed him, magnifying God. And all the people as they saw it, gave praise to God.
