7:1
And when he had fully said all his words into the ears of the people, he entered into Capharnaum.

7:2
And the servant of a certain Centurion being sick, was ready to die:
who was dear unto him.

7:3
And when he had heard of Jesus, he sent unto him the Ancients of the Jews, desiring him to come and heal his servant.

7:4
But they being come to Jesus, besought him earnestly, saying to him,
That he is worthy that thou shouldest do this for him.

7:5
For he loveth our Nation;
and he hath built a Synagogue for us.

7:6
And Jesus went with them.
And when he was now not far from the house, the Centurion sent his friends unto him, saying:
Lord, trouble not thyself.
For I am not worthy that thou shouldest enter under my roof.

7:7
For the which cause neither did I think myself worthy
to come to thee;
but say the word,
and my servant shall be made whole.

7:8
For I also am a man subject to authority, having under me soldiers:
and I say to this,
go,
and he goeth:
and to another,
come,
and he cometh;
and to my servant,
do this,
and he doeth it.

7:9
Which Jesus hearing, marveled;
and turning to the multitudes that followed him he said:
Amen I say to you,
neither in Israel have I found so great faith.

7:10
And they that were sent, being returned home, found the servant that had been sick, whole.

7:11
And it came to pass,
afterward he went into a city that is called Naim;
and there went with him his Disciples and a very great multitude.

7:12
And when he came nigh to the gate of the city, behold a dead man was carried forth, the only son of his mother;
and she was a widow:
and a great multitude of the city with her.

7:13
Whom when our Lord had seen, being moved with mercy upon her, he said to her:
Weep not.

7:14
And he came near
and touched the coffin.
And they that carried it stood still;
and he said:
Young man, I say to thee,
arise.

7:15
And he that was dead, sat up,
and began to speak.
And he gave him to his mother.

7:16
And fear took them all;
and they magnified God, saying,
That a great Prophet is risen among us:
and, that God hath visited his people.

7:17
And this saying went forth into all Jewry of him, & into all the country about.

7:18
And John's disciples shewed him of all these things.

7:19
And John called two of his disciples,
and sent them to Jesus, saying:
Art thou he that art to come;
or expect we another?

7:20
And when the men were come unto him, they said:
John the Baptist hath sent us to thee, saying:
Art thou he that art to come;
or expect we another?

7:21
(And the self same hour, he cured many of maladies, and hurts, and evil spirits:
and to many blind he gave sight.)

7:22
And answering, he said to them:
Go & report to John what you have heard and seen: That the blind see,
the lame walk,
the lepers are made clean,
the deaf hear,
the dead rise again,


7:23
the poor are evangelized: and blessed is he whosoever shall not be scandalized in me.

7:24
And when John's messengers were departed, he began to say of John to the multitudes:
What went you out into the desert to see?
A reed moved with the wind?

7:25
But what went you forth to see?
A man clothed in soft garments?
Behold they that are in costly apparel and delicacies, are in the house of kings.

7:26
But what went you out for to see?
A Prophet?
Certes I say to you,
and more than a Prophet.

7:27
This is he of whom it is written:
Behold I send mine Angel before thy face, which shall prepare thy way before thee.

7:28
For I say to you:
A greater Prophet among the children of women than John the Baptist, there is no man.
But he that is the lesser in the Kingdom of God, is greater than he.

7:29
And all the people hearing and the Publicans, justified God, being baptized with John's baptism.

7:30
But the Pharisees and the lawyers despised the counsel of God against themselves, being not baptized of him.

7:31
And our Lord said: Where unto then shall I liken the men of this generation,
and whereunto are they like?

7:32
They are like to children sitting in the market-place, and speaking one to another, and saying:
We have piped to you,
and you have not danced;
we have lamented,
and you have not wept.

7:33
For John the Baptist came neither eating bread nor drinking wine;
and you say:
He hath a devil.

7:34
The Son of man came eating and drinking;
and you say:
Behold a man that is a gourmander and a drinker of wine, a friend of Publicans and sinners.

7:35
And wisdom is justified of all her children.

7:36
And one of the Pharisees desired him to eat with him.
And being entered into the house of the Pharisee, he sat down to meat.

7:37
And behold a woman that was in the city, a sinner, as she knew that he was set down in the Pharisee's house, she brought an alabaster box of ointment;

7:38
and standing behind beside his feet, she began to water his feet with tears,
& wiped them with the hairs of her head,
and kissed his feet,
& anointed them with the ointment.

7:39
And the Pharisee that had bid him, seeing it, spake within himself, saying:
This man if he were a Prophet, would know certes who and what manner of woman she is which toucheth him, that she is a sinner.

7:40
And Jesus answering said to him:
Simon, I have somewhat to say unto thee.
But he said:
Master, say.

7:41
Acertain creditor had two debters;
one did owe five hundred pence, & the other fifty.

7:42
They having not wherewith to pay, he forgave both.
Whether therefore doth love him more?

7:43
Simon answering said:
I suppose that he to whom he forgave more.
But he said to him:
Thou hast judged rightly.

7:44
And turning to the woman, he said unto Simon:
Doest thou see this woman?
I entered into thy house,
water to my feet thou didst not give;
but she with tears hath watered my feet,
and with her hairs hath wiped them.

7:45
Kiss thou gavest me not;
but she since I came in, hath not ceased to kiss my feet.

7:46
With oil thou didst not anoint my head;
but she with ointment hath anointed my feet.

7:47
For the which I say to thee:
Many sins are forgiven her, because she hath loved much.
But to whom less is forgiven, he loveth less.

7:48
And he said to her:
Thy sins are forgiven thee.

7:49
And they that sat together at the table began to say within themselves.
Who is this that also forgiveth sins?

7:50
And he said to the woman:
Thy faith hath made thee safe,
go in peace.
