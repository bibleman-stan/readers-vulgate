17:1
And he said to his Disciples:
It is impossible that scandals should not come:
but woe to him by whom they come.

17:2
It is more profitable for him,
if a mill-stone be put about his neck,
and he be cast into the sea,
than that he scandalize one of these little ones.

17:3
Look well to yourselves.
If thy brother sin against thee, rebuke him:
and if he do penance, forgive him.

17:4
And if he sin against thee seven times in a day, and seven times in a day be converted unto thee, saying,
It repenteth me,
forgive him.

17:5
And the Apostles said to our Lord:
Increase faith in us.

17:6
And our Lord said:
If you had faith like to a mustard-seed, you might say to this mulberry tree,
be thou rooted up,
and be transplanted into the sea:
and it would obey you.

17:7
And which of you having a servant plowing or keeping cattle, that will say to him returning out of the field:
Pass quickly,
sit down:

17:8
and saith not to him:
Make ready supper,
and gird thyself,
and serve me whiles I eat and drink,
and afterward thou shalt eat and drink?




17:9
Doth he give that servant thanks,
for doing the things which he commanded him?

17:10
Itrow not: So you also, when you shall have done all things that are commanded you, say:
We are unprofitable servants;
we have done that which we ought to do.

17:11
And it came to pass,
as he went unto Jerusalem,
he passed through the midst of Samaria and Galilee.

17:12
And when he entered into a certain town, there met him ten men that were lepers, who stood a far off;

17:13
and they lifted up their voice, saying:
Jesus Master, have mercy on us.

17:14
Whom as he saw, he said:
Go,
shew yourselves to the Priests.
And it came to pass,
as they went,
they were made clean.

17:15
And one of them as he saw that he was made clean,
went back with a loud voice magnifying God,

17:16
and he fell on his face before his feet, giving thanks: and this was a Samaritane.

17:17
And Jesus answering said:
Were not ten made clean? and where are the nine?


17:18
There was not found that returned, and gave glory to God, but this stranger.

17:19
And he said to him:
Arise,
go thy ways;
because thy faith hath made thee safe.

17:20
And being asked of the Pharisees:
when cometh the Kingdom of God? he answered them and said:
The Kingdom of God cometh not with observation:

17:21
neither shall they say:
Lo here,
or lo there.
For lo the Kingdom of God is within you.


17:22
And he said to his Disciples:
The days will come when you shall desire to see one day of the Son of man;
and you shall not see.

17:23
And they will say to you:
Lo here and lo there.
Go not,
neither do ye follow after.


17:24
For even as the lightning that lighteneth from under Heaven, unto those parts that are under Heaven, shineth: so shall the Son of man be in his day.

17:25
But first he must suffer many things and be rejected of this generation.

17:26
And as it came to pass in the days of Noe, so shall it be also in the days of the Son of man.

17:27
They did eat and drink,
they did marry wives and were given to marriage even until the day that Noe entered into the ark:
and the flood came,
and destroyed them all.



17:28
Likewise as it came to pass in the days of Lot:
They did eat and drink,
bought and sold,
planted,
and builded:



17:29
and in the day that Lot went out from Sodom, it rained fire & brimstone from Heaven,
& destroyed them all:

17:30
according to these things it shall be in the day that the Son of man shall be revealed.

17:31
In that hour he that shall be in the house-top, and his vessels in the house, let him not go down to take them up:
& he that is in the field, in like manner let him not return back.

17:32
Be mindful of Lot's wife.

17:33
Whosoever seeketh to save his life, shall lose it:
and Whosoever doth lose the same, shall quicken it.

17:34
Isay to you,
in that night there shall be two in one bed:
the one shall be taken,
and the other shall be left:

17:35
two women shall be grinding together:
the one shall be taken,
and the other shall be left.
two in the field:
the one shall be taken,
and the other shall be left.

17:36
They answering say to him:
Where Lord?

17:37
Who said to them:
Wheresoever the body shall be, thither will the eagles also be gathered together.
