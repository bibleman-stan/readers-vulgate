23:1
And all the multitude of them rising up, led him to Pilate.

23:2
And they began to accuse him, saying:
We have found this man subverting our nation, & prohibiting to give tributes to Cæsar, and saying that he is Christ the King.

23:3
And Pilate asked him, saying:
Art thou the King of the Jews?
But he answering said:
Thou sayest.

23:4
And Pilate said to the chief Priests and multitudes:
I find no cause in this man.

23:5
But they were more earnest, saying:
He stirreth the people teaching throughout all Jewry, beginning from Galilee even hither.

23:6
But Pilate hearing Galilee, asked if the man were of Galilee.

23:7
And when he understood that he was of Herod's jurisdiction, he sent him back to Herod, who was also himself at Jerusalem in those days.

23:8
And Herod seeing Jesus, was very glad,
for he was desirous of a long time to see him,
for because he heard many things of him:
and he hoped to see some sign wrought by him.

23:9
And he asked him in many words.
But he answered him nothing.

23:10
And there stood the chief Priests and the Scribes constantly accusing him.

23:11
And Herod with his army set him at naught:
and he mocked him, putting on him a white garment,
and sent him back to Pilate.

23:12
And Herod and Pilate were made friends that day.
For before they were enemies one to another.

23:13
And Pilate calling together the chief Priests and Magistrates, and the people,

23:14
said to them: You have presented unto me this man, as averting the people,
and behold
I examining him before you, have found no cause in this man of those things, wherein you accuse him.

23:15
No, nor Herod neither.
For I sent you to him,
and behold,
nothing worthy of death is done to him.

23:16
I will chasten him therefore and dismiss him.

23:17
And he of necessity had to release unto them upon the feast day, one.

23:18
But the whole multitude together cried out, saying:
Dispatch him,
and release us Barabbas.

23:19
Who was for a certain sedition made in the city and murder, cast into prison.

23:20
And Pilate again spake to them, desirous to release Jesus.

23:21
But they cried again, saying:
Crucify,
crucify him.

23:22
And he the third time said to them:
Why,
what evil hath this man done? I find no cause of death in him.
I will correct him therefore
& let him go.

23:23
But they were instant with loud voices requiring that he might be crucified.
And their voices prevailed.

23:24
And Pilate adjudged their petition to be done.

23:25
And he released unto them him that for murder and sedition had been cast into prison, whom they demanded:
but Jesus he delivered to their pleasure.

23:26
And when they led him, they took one Simon of Cyrene coming from the country:
and they laid the Cross upon him to carry after Jesus.

23:27
And there followed him a great multitude of people, and of women which bewailed and lamented him.

23:28
But Jesus turning to them, said:
Daughters of Jerusalem, weep not upon me,
but weep upon yourselves, and upon your children.



23:29
For behold the days shall come, wherein they will say: Blessed are the barren, and the wombs that have not born, & the paps that have not given suck.

23:30
Then shall they begin to say to the mountains,
Fall upon us:
and to the hills,
Cover us.

23:31
For if in the green wood they do these things, in the dry what shall be done?

23:32
And there were led also other two malefactors with him,
to be executed.

23:33
And after they came to the place which is called Calvary, there they crucified him:
and the thieves, one on the right hand and the other on the left.

23:34
And Jesus said:
Father,
forgive them, for they know not what they do.
But they dividing his garments, did cast lots.

23:35
And the people stood beholding,
and the Princes with them derided him, saying:
Others he hath saved,
let him save himself, if this be Christ, the elect of God.

23:36
And the soldiers also mocked him coming to him, and offering him vinegar,

23:37
saying: If thou be the King of the Jews, save thyself.

23:38
And there was also a superscription written over him in Greek, and Latin, and Hebrew letters:
This is The King of The Jews.

23:39
And one of those thieves that were hanged, blasphemed him, saying:
If thou be Christ, save thyself, and us.

23:40
But the other answering, rebuked him, saying:
Neither doest thou fear God, where as thou art in the same damnation?

23:41
And we indeed justly,
for we receive worthy of our doings:
but this man hath done no evil.

23:42
And he said to Jesus:
Lord,
remember me when thou shalt come into thy Kingdom.

23:43
And Jesus said to him:
Amen I say to thee;
this day thou shalt be with me in Paradise.

23:44
And it was almost the sixth hour:
and there was made darkness upon the whole earth until the ninth hour.

23:45
And the sun was darkened;
and the veil of the temple was rent in the midst.

23:46
And Jesus crying with a loud voice, said:
Father, into thy hands I commend my Spirit.
And saying this, he gave up the ghost.

23:47
And the Centurion seeing that which was done, glorified God, saying:
Indeed this man was just.

23:48
And all the multitude of them that were present together at that sight, and saw the things that were done, returned knocking their breasts.

23:49
And all his acquaintance stood a far off, and the women that had followed him from Galilee, seeing these things.

23:50
And behold a man named Joseph, which was a Senator, a good man and a just.

23:51
He had not consented to their counsel and doings, of Arimathæa a city of Jewry, who also himself expected the Kingdom of God.

23:52
This man came to Pilate,
and asked the body of Jesus.

23:53
And taking it down, wrapped it in sindon,
and laid him in a monument hewed of stone, wherein never yet any man had been laid.

23:54
And it was the day of Parasceve,
and the Sabboth drew near.

23:55
And the women that were come with him from Galilee, following after, saw the monument, and how his body was laid.

23:56
And returning they prepared spices and ointments:
and on the Sabboth they rested according to the commandment.
