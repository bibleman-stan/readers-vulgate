1:9
Thou hast loved justice, and hated iniquity: therefore thee, God, thy God hath anointed with the oil of exultation above thy fellows. And, Thou in the beginning o Lord didst found the earth: and the works of thy hands are the heavens. They shall perish, but thou shalt continue: and they shall all wax old as a garment. And as a vesture shalt thou change them, and they shall be changed: but thou art the self-same, and thy years shall not fail. But to which of the Angels said he at any time: Sit on my right hand, until I make thine enemies the foot-stool of thy feet? Are they not all, ministering spirits: sent to minister for them which shall receive the inheritance of salvation?
