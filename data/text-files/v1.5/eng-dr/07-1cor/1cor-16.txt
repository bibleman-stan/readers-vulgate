16:1
And concerning the collections that are made for the Saints, as I have ordained to the Churches of Galatia, so do ye also.

16:2
In the first of the Sabboth let every one of you put a part with himself, laying up what shall well like him: that not when I come, then collections be made.


16:3
And when I shall be present; whom you shall approve by letters, them will I send to carry your grace into Jerusalem.

16:4
And if it be worthy that I also go, they shall go with me.

16:5
And I will come to you, when I shall have passed through Macedonia. For I will pass through Macedonia.



16:6
And with you perhaps I will abide, or will winter also: that you may bring me on my way whithersoever I go.




16:7
For I will not now see you by the way, for I hope that I shall abide with you some little time, if our Lord will permit.



16:8
But I will tarry at Ephesus until Pentecost.

16:9
For a great door and evident is opened unto me: and many adversaries.

16:10
And if Timothee come, see that he be without fear with you, for he worketh the work of our Lord, as also I.


16:11
Let no man therefore despise him, but conduct ye him in peace: that he may come to me. For I expect him with the brethren.




16:12
And of brother Apollo I do you to understand, that I much entreated him to come unto you with the brethren: and at all it was not his mind to come now. But he will come when he shall have leisure.




16:13
Watch ye, stand in the faith, do manfully, & be strengthened.




16:14
Let all your things be done in charity.

16:15
And I beseech you, brethren, you know the house of Stephanas, and of Fortunatus, that they are the first fruits of Achaia, & have ordained themselves to the ministry of the saints:




16:17
And I rejoice in the presence of Stephanas and Fortunatus and Achaicus, because that which you wanted, they have supplied.


16:18
For they have refreshed both my spirit and yours. Know them therefore that are such.


16:19
The Churches of Asia salute you. Aquila and Priscilla with their domestical Church salute you much in our Lord.


16:20
All the brethren salute you. Salute one another in a holy kiss.


16:21
The salutation with mine own hand Paul's.

16:22
If any man love not our Lord Jesus Christ, be he anathema. Maran atha.


16:23
The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with you.

16:24
My charity be with you all in Christ Jesus. Amen.
