2:1
And I (brethren) when I came to you, I came not in loftiness of speech or of wisdom, preaching to you the testimony of Christ.

2:2
For I judged not myself to know any thing among you but Jesus Christ, and him crucified.

2:3
And I was with you in infirmity, and fear and much trembling:

2:4
and my speech and my preaching was not in the persuasible words of human wisdom, but in shewing of spirit and power; that your faith might not be in the wisdom of men, but in the power of God. But we speak wisdom among the perfect.

2:6
But the wisdom not of this world,
neither of the princes of this world, that come to naught:

2:7
but we speak the wisdom of God in a mystery, which is hid, which God did predestinate before the worlds, unto our glory:

2:8
which none of the princes of this world did know: for if they had known, they would never have crucified the Lord of glory.

2:9
But as it is written:
That which eye hath not seen,
nor ear hath heard,
neither hath it ascended into the heart of man, what things God hath prepared for them that love him.

2:10
But to us God hath revealed by his Spirit. For the Spirit searcheth all things, yea the profundities of God.

2:11
For what man knoweth the things of a man, but the spirit of a man that is in him? so the things also that are of God no man knoweth,
but the spirit of God.

2:12
And we have received not the spirit of this world, but the spirit that is of God:
that we may know the things that of God are given to us.

2:13
Which also we speak not in learned words of human wisdom; but in the doctrine of the Spirit, comparing spiritual things to the spiritual.

2:14
But the sensual man perceiveth not those things that are of the Spirit of God.
For it is foolishness to him, and he cannot understand;
because he is spiritually examined.

2:15
But the spiritual man judgeth all things:
and himself is judged of no man.

2:16
For who hath known the sense of our Lord that may instruct him? But we have the sense of Christ.
