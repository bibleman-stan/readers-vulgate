1:1
Paul and Timothee the servants of Jesus Christ; to all the saints in Christ Jesus that are at Philippi, with the Bishops and Deacons.

1:2
Grace to you and peace from God our Father, and our Lord Jesus Christ.

1:3
I give thanks to my God in all memory of you

1:7
As it is reason for me, this to think for all you, for that I have you in heart, & in my bands, and in the defence, and the confirmation of the Gospel, all you to be partakers of my joy.


1:8
For God is my witness, how I covet you all in the bowels of Jesus Christ.

1:12
And I will have you know, brethren, that the things about me are come to the more furtherance of the Gospel:



1:15
Some indeed even for envy and contention: but some also for good will preach Christ.


1:16
Some of charity: knowing that I am set unto the defence of the Gospel.

1:17
And some of contention preach Christ not sincerely: supposing that they raise affliction to my bands.

1:18
But what? So that by all means, whether by occasion, or by truth, Christ be preached: in this also I rejoice, yea & will rejoice.



1:21
For unto me, to live is Christ: and to die is gain.

1:22
And if to live in the flesh, this unto me be the fruit of the work, and what I shall choose I know not.


1:23
And I am straitened of the two: having desire to be dissolved & to be with Christ, a thing much more better.

1:24
But to abide in the flesh, necessary for you.

1:25
And trusting this, I know that I shall abide and continue with you all, unto your furtherance and joy of the faith:
